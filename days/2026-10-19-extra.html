A Seat at the Screening

在放映厅里，听见一部电影

先顺着人物的行动读完故事，再回头猜词。长按单词可标记或取消绿色掌握状态。

The film club had an auditorium that afternoon, courtesy of a local cinema. Lin arrived for the rehearsal with a script; Mei, a peer from the film club, carried the headphones. A technician opened a short excerpt from Departure. Four regular filmgoers with a visual impairment had come to listen. Lin would add narration between the spoken lines. On screen, a father was seeing his daughter off at a railway station.

🟣 表达拆解
courtesy of 表示承蒙提供；between the spoken lines 指对白间隙；see somebody off 是送行，不是看见某人离开。
🧠 段落回忆：谁在准备什么活动？片段里正在发生什么？
提示1 · 逻辑链
影院准备→观众试听→交代片中的送别场景。
提示2 · 猜词线索
script、headphones、spoken lines 共同提示 narration 的用途；for the rehearsal 与试听相呼应。
显示答案 · 本段词义
auditorium：礼堂；观众厅；rehearsal：排练；试演；narration：叙述；解说；excerpt：摘录；片段；impairment：损伤；功能障碍；courtesy：承蒙；由……提供（courtesy of）；peer：同龄人；同辈；technician：技术人员

Lin had written pages of eloquent prose. His portrayal of the father was tender, and his account of the departure grew increasingly sentimental. When he finished, Mrs Zhou thanked him with a gracious smile. Then she asked where the daughter had put her suitcase. He had to confess that he had left it out. Two sentences about sorrow suddenly looked superfluous beside that missing case.

🟣 表达拆解
left it out 指省略了行李箱的位置；beside 在末句表示相较之下，用具体遗漏反衬前面的抒情。
🧠 段落回忆：周女士的问题，暴露出初稿的什么缺口？
提示1 · 逻辑链
文字动人→观众追问一个具体物件→发现关键信息没交代。
提示2 · 猜词线索
pages of... prose 与 two sentences about sorrow 提示文风；missing case 说明为什么那些抒情显得 superfluous。
显示答案 · 本段词义
eloquent：有说服力的；富于文采的；prose：散文；非韵文文字；sentimental：感伤的；多愁善感的；portrayal：描绘；刻画；superfluous：多余的；不必要的；confess：坦白；承认；gracious：宽厚的；有礼的

An awkward pause followed. Lin turned a page to hide his embarrassment. "What else is missing?" he asked, trying not to sound defensive. Mrs Zhou was candid: she could hear a train but could not tell whether the father was inside it. Lin watched again. That omission affected the whole scene. He replaced "an affectionate gesture" with a literal description: "On the platform, he straightens the collar of her coat."

🟣 表达拆解
trying not to... 是伴随动作；whether 区分父亲在车内还是车外；replaced A with B 给出修改前后的具体对照。
🧠 段落回忆：林怎样把抽象的动作评价改成观众能理解的画面？
提示1 · 逻辑链
不知道人物位置→重新看片→写清位置与动作。
提示2 · 猜词线索
could not tell 与 omission 对应；两段引语对照 gesture 的笼统与 literal description 的具体。
显示答案 · 本段词义
awkward：尴尬的；不自在的；embarrassment：尴尬；窘迫；omission：遗漏；省略；gesture：手势；姿态；动作；literal：按字面或实际情况的；不加引申的；candid：坦诚的；直率的；defensive：防御性的；戒备的

They replayed the departure. With meticulous care, Lin noted the case beside the carriage door and the ticket in the daughter's hand. He tried to articulate each action in a concise phrase. An elaborate description of the clock and other trivial details had to go. Yet one subtlety survived in the verbal script: the father kept smoothing a collar that was already straight.

🟣 表达拆解
had to go 在这里指必须删去；that was already straight 修饰 collar，使重复整理的动作有了分量。
🧠 段落回忆：删掉一些细节之后，哪一个小动作仍然值得留下？
提示1 · 逻辑链
明确位置和物件→压缩篇幅→留下能表现人物的小动作。
提示2 · 猜词线索
already straight 与 kept smoothing 的反差体现 subtlety；no time 提供 concise、trivial 的取舍线索。
显示答案 · 本段词义
concise：简明的；简洁的；elaborate：详尽的；精心铺陈的；verbal：口头的；用语言表达的；trivial：琐碎的；不重要的；subtlety：微妙之处；细微差别；meticulous：细心的；一丝不苟的；articulate：清楚表达

In the next scene, the daughter said, "Very helpful," with an inflection that made her sarcasm obvious. Lin's voice had covered it, along with the piano accompaniment. A nuance implicit in the actor's delivery disappeared. On the next attempt, he tried to enunciate every word with equal weight. The result was monotonous. Mrs Zhou interrupted his next utterance. "I can hear you," she said. "You can speak normally."

🟣 表达拆解
along with 表示同时被盖住的还有伴奏；with equal weight 指每个词都同样用力；normally 与前面的刻意咬字形成对照。
🧠 段落回忆：这一轮试听中，林的声音怎样影响了原片？
提示1 · 逻辑链
盖住演员语调与音乐→过分纠正咬字→观众要求自然说话。
提示2 · 猜词线索
Very helpful 与 sarcasm 说明语调能改变字面意思；equal weight 为 monotonous 提供听觉线索。
显示答案 · 本段词义
inflection：语调的变化；nuance：细微差别；微妙意味；enunciate：清楚地发音；清晰表达；monotonous：单调的；缺少变化的；utterance：话语；说出的话；accompaniment：伴奏；伴随物；implicit：含蓄的；未明说的；sarcasm：讥讽；挖苦

Next came a stretch without dialogue. Lin's impulse was to fill it. "A poignant farewell—" he began. A suitcase wheel scraped the platform; paper began to rustle inside a pocket. Mrs Zhou leaned forward. "Wait. I want to hear that," she said in a whisper. Lin had to refrain from speaking. He crossed out the phrase and left the sounds intact. Mrs Zhou kept listening, waiting in suspense.

🟣 表达拆解
Next came... 用倒装推进时间；that 指原片里刚出现的声音；refrain from speaking 表示克制开口；left the sounds intact 表示保留原片声音。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么这段没有对白，林反而不该急着说话？
提示1 · 逻辑链
想填满空隙→观众正听声音、等后续→删掉提前下的结论。
提示2 · 猜词线索
scraped、paper... inside a pocket 帮助猜 rustle；leaned forward、kept listening 提供 suspense 的反应线索；crossed out 与 intact 构成取舍。
显示答案 · 本段词义
rustle：发出窸窣声；whisper：低语；耳语；suspense：悬念；焦急的等待；poignant：令人心酸的；深深动人的；impulse：冲动；推动力；refrain：克制；避免做；intact：完整无损的

By the last take, Lin's narration was fluent and natural. He kept the description impartial, without making it flat. Once he slipped into a patronizing tone; Mrs Zhou repeated the line in his tone, and everyone laughed. There was little improvisation now. He could salvage a few phrases from his first draft. "Will you join our next consultation?" he asked. "I'm already your editor," she said, tapping the transcript.

🟣 表达拆解
without making it flat 区分不擅自评判与读得平板；slipped into 指不知不觉进入某种语气；in his tone 指用林刚才的语气复述，回应前面的 patronizing tone。
🧠 段落回忆：到了最后一遍，周女士与林是怎样一起工作的？
提示1 · 逻辑链
语气自然→用模仿指出问题→共同确定稿件。
提示2 · 猜词线索
read the line back 与 everyone laughed 具体展示 patronizing 的问题；first draft 与 salvage 表示从旧稿保留有用部分。
显示答案 · 本段词义
fluent：流利的；流畅的；impartial：公正的；不偏不倚的；patronizing：以恩人自居的；居高临下的；improvisation：即兴发挥；transcript：文字记录；文字稿；consultation：协商；咨询；salvage：挽救；保留有用部分

On Saturday, a queue formed in the foyer. During the final inspection, the technician made a deft adjustment to a receiver while Lin checked its controls against the manual. Mrs Zhou greeted an acquaintance, then opened her bag to retrieve her headphones. Lin waited by the microphone. His mouth was dry with anticipation. Mei put a glass of water beside his script.

🟣 表达拆解
against the manual 表示对照说明书；went to retrieve 指去取回设备；while 让两项准备工作同时发生。
🧠 段落回忆：正式放映前，哪些细节让你感到活动真的要开始了？
提示1 · 逻辑链
观众排队→设备检查→林坐到话筒前。
提示2 · 猜词线索
queue 提示 foyer 是观众入场前的空间；receiver、controls 提供 inspection 和 adjustment 的实物场景。
显示答案 · 本段词义
foyer：门厅；休息厅；acquaintance：熟人；相识的人；anticipation：期待；预期；deft：熟练灵巧的；inspection：检查；检验；manual：使用说明书；retrieve：取回；找回；queue：队列

A hush settled over the room. The daughter tugged her father's sleeve, tied a crimson scarf around his neck, and boarded. Lin waited for his cue. "She puts her palm against the window. On the platform, her father raises his hand to meet hers." The train moved. His solitary silhouette grew smaller beyond the glass. A flash of light gave Lin a glimpse of Mrs Zhou's smile.

🟣 表达拆解
to meet hers 中 hers 指女儿的手；beyond the glass 指车窗外；grew smaller 随火车开动交代空间变化。
🧠 段落回忆：送别的结尾用了哪些动作？观众的反应怎样呈现出来？
提示1 · 逻辑链
系围巾→上车→隔窗举手→火车开动→观众微笑。
提示2 · 猜词线索
tugged 与 father's 提示 sleeve 是衣物部位；raises his hand 对应 hers；flash of light 与 glimpse 表示短暂看见。
显示答案 · 本段词义
hush：安静；寂静；sleeve：袖子；cue：提示；行动信号；solitary：独自的；孤单的；silhouette：剪影；轮廓；glimpse：一瞥；短暂看见；crimson：深红色；深红的

After the credits, Mrs Zhou began talking about her father. He used to wait until her train disappeared before leaving the platform. Her voice softened with nostalgia. Lin scarcely noticed Mei collecting the equipment. "What's on next week?" Mrs Zhou asked. He read out the title. "A comedy," he added. Her answer was emphatic: "Book me a seat." He reached for the reservation list, and she asked who was in the cast.

🟣 表达拆解
used to 指过去经常做的事；until... before... 强调父亲等待的终点；What's on 询问正在或即将上演什么；he added 补充电影类型，接到订座的行动。
🧠 段落回忆：放映之后，两人谈了什么？故事停在了哪个具体行动上？
提示1 · 逻辑链
谈起父亲→介绍下一部电影→订座并询问演员。
提示2 · 猜词线索
used to wait 和父亲的往事提示 nostalgia；Book me a seat 与 reservation 直接呼应；scarcely noticed 表示几乎没注意。
显示答案 · 本段词义
nostalgia：怀旧；对往事的怀念；scarcely：几乎不；勉强；reservation：预订；预约；emphatic：强调的；坚决有力的
