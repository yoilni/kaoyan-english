By four o'clock, the auditorium would be full. Lin had arrived early for a rehearsal, courtesy of a local cinema that had lent its space to the university film club. His peer Mei checked the reservation list while a technician played an excerpt of recorded narration through the headphones. The club was hosting a screening for people with a visual impairment. Lin had never welcomed an audience before.

🟣 表达拆解
By four o’clock... would... 从林提前到场的时刻展望四点；courtesy of 交代场地由谁提供；while 连接同时进行的两项准备。
🧠 段落回忆：放映前，林、梅和技术人员分别在做什么？
提示1 · 逻辑链
提前到场→核对名单、测试声音→林面临第一次开场。
提示2 · 猜词线索
lent its space 提示 courtesy of 的关系；headphones 与 recorded 提示解说通过什么方式传给观众。
显示答案 · 本段词义

His welcome was three pages long. He had worked hard on the prose, hoping to sound eloquent and fluent. Mei stopped halfway through his sentimental portrayal of the guests and their daily struggles. He had hoped a poignant sentence about courage would move them. 'They've booked a film, Lin,' she said. He looked at the empty seats, then crossed it out. The elaborate opening suddenly seemed superfluous. He could still salvage the practical information.

🟣 表达拆解
stopped halfway through... 指读到一半停下；crossed it out 中 it 指前面的那句关于勇气的话；could still salvage 表示删改后仍能保留有用部分。
🧠 段落回忆：梅的提醒怎样改变了林对欢迎词的看法？
提示1 · 逻辑链
精心准备长稿→同伴提醒观众来意→删去抒情、留下实用信息。
提示2 · 猜词线索
three pages long 与 practical information 形成取舍；crossed it out 帮助判断 superfluous；daily struggles、courage 提示文稿的感伤色彩。
显示答案 · 本段词义
eloquent：有说服力的；富于文采的；prose：散文；非韵文文字；sentimental：感伤的；多愁善感的；portrayal：描绘；刻画；superfluous：多余的；不必要的；elaborate：详尽的；精心铺陈的；poignant：令人心酸的；深深动人的；fluent：流利的；流畅的；salvage：挽救；保留有用部分

Mrs Zhou arrived wearing a crimson coat, one sleeve still damp from the rain. Lin checked her name and said, 'We've kept the front row for you.' 'Can I choose where I sit?' she asked. An awkward silence followed. He had to confess that he had put all the guests together so volunteers could find them. He could feel his embarrassment rising. Mrs Zhou waited for an answer.

🟣 表达拆解
one sleeve still damp... 补充衣服到场时的状态；kept... for you 表示预留；so volunteers could... 交代林安排座位的实际出发点。
🧠 段落回忆：周女士问了什么？林原先为什么把观众安排在一起？
提示1 · 逻辑链
预留第一排→观众要求自己选→林说出安排的原因。
提示2 · 猜词线索
silence、waited for an answer 与 rising 一起呈现窘迫；coat 提供 sleeve 的衣物线索。
显示答案 · 本段词义
awkward：尴尬的；不自在的；embarrassment：尴尬；窘迫；sleeve：袖子；confess：坦白；承认；crimson：深红色；深红的

His first impulse was to explain. He had arranged the seating chart with meticulous care and thought the plan impartial: everyone got a front-row seat. But he managed to refrain from defending it. 'Of course. Where would you like to go?' Mrs Zhou was candid. She found the front too loud. Her tone was gracious, but her answer was emphatic: 'Halfway back, please.' No sarcasm, no argument. Lin stopped feeling defensive.

🟣 表达拆解
thought the plan impartial 中 impartial 是他当时对安排的判断；冒号后说明他把同样安排在第一排当作公平；refrain from defending it 中 it 指座位安排。
🧠 段落回忆：林为什么想解释？周女士怎样说清了自己的选择？
提示1 · 逻辑链
想为安排辩护→忍住解释→听见具体偏好→放下戒备。
提示2 · 猜词线索
everyone got a front-row seat 呈现林对 impartial 的理解；please 与明确的座位要求共同提示 gracious、emphatic 可以同时成立。
显示答案 · 本段词义
impartial：公正的；不偏不倚的；emphatic：强调的；坚决有力的；gracious：宽厚的；有礼的；candid：坦诚的；直率的；defensive：防御性的；戒备的；meticulous：细心的；一丝不苟的；sarcasm：讥讽；挖苦；impulse：冲动；推动力；refrain：克制；避免做

At the rear aisle, his gesture towards an empty chair told her little. He tried to articulate the route in concise verbal directions: 'Three rows down.' 'Towards the screen?' she asked. He had missed that subtlety. 'Yes, towards the screen, then two seats to your left.' The literal description worked. She sat down and asked him to keep the next seat free for an acquaintance. He left the other reservations intact.

🟣 表达拆解
towards... 说清移动方向；keep the next seat free 表示暂时留空；left... intact 表示不改变其余预约，不是取消别人的座位。
🧠 段落回忆：林的指路哪里不够清楚？他后来怎样说清了座位位置？
提示1 · 逻辑链
手势不起作用→口头方向仍有歧义→补清方向与座位数→顺利落座。
提示2 · 猜词线索
Three rows down 与 Towards the screen 的问答呈现 subtlety；指出空椅子与 verbal directions 对照手势和语言。
显示答案 · 本段词义
gesture：手势；姿态；动作；literal：按字面或实际情况的；不加引申的；concise：简明的；简洁的；verbal：口头的；用语言表达的；subtlety：微妙之处；细微差别；acquaintance：熟人；相识的人；articulate：清楚表达；intact：完整无损的

The technician brought over a receiver for a final inspection. Lin checked the controls against the manual and made a deft adjustment. Mrs Zhou listened, then lifted one side of the headphones. The commentary was clear, but the musical accompaniment sounded faint. Lin had dismissed the balance as a trivial detail. Now he asked the technician to adjust it. He waited in suspense while she tried again. She nodded. He noted her setting.

🟣 表达拆解
against the manual 指对照说明书；had dismissed... as... 交代他此前没有重视什么；while she tried again 让等待与试听同时发生。
🧠 段落回忆：周女士试听后提出了什么问题？他们怎样确认调整有效？
提示1 · 逻辑链
检查设备→发现声音比例不合适→重新调整→观众试听确认。
提示2 · 猜词线索
clear 与 faint 对照两种声音；waited... while she tried again 为 suspense 提供等待结果的情境。
显示答案 · 本段词义
trivial：琐碎的；不重要的；accompaniment：伴奏；伴随物；suspense：悬念；焦急的等待；deft：熟练灵巧的；technician：技术人员；inspection：检查；检验；manual：使用说明书

Lin tried his welcome at the microphone. He made himself enunciate each word, but every utterance came out at the same pace. 'You sound a little monotonous,' Mrs Zhou said. He began again, more slowly. 'Not more slowly. More like you.' A playful inflection softened the criticism. He caught the nuance and laughed. Her smile offered implicit reassurance. He tried again, dropping the patronizing emphasis.

🟣 表达拆解
made himself... 表示有意要求自己做某事；More like you 省略了 speak，回应他刻意改变的说话方式；dropping... 是伴随动作，说明再次试讲时怎样调整语气。
🧠 段落回忆：林把问题理解成了什么？听懂回应后，他怎样调整？
提示1 · 逻辑链
刻意咬字→节奏单调→误以为要更慢→听懂回应、自然试讲。
提示2 · 猜词线索
at the same pace 为 monotonous 提供听觉线索；playful... softened 说明语调的作用；smile 与 reassurance 提供 implicit 的非言语线索。
显示答案 · 本段词义
inflection：语调的变化；nuance：细微差别；微妙意味；enunciate：清楚地发音；清晰表达；monotonous：单调的；缺少变化的；utterance：话语；说出的话；patronizing：以恩人自居的；居高临下的；implicit：含蓄的；未明说的

From the foyer came the rustle of coats and the hum of a queue. Mei went to retrieve a spare receiver. Lin asked Mrs Zhou whether her friend had arrived. She answered in a whisper: 'Not yet.' At the door, he caught a glimpse of a solitary silhouette against the bright foyer. 'Zhou?' the man called. She raised a hand. Lin went to meet him and brought him to the empty seat.

🟣 表达拆解
From the foyer came... 把声音来源放在句首；whether... had arrived 问的是此前是否已经到场；against the bright foyer 交代人影的明亮背景。
🧠 段落回忆：周女士预留的座位后来坐进了谁？林怎样接上了这次迎接？
提示1 · 逻辑链

提示2 · 猜词线索
against the bright foyer 提示 silhouette 是背光轮廓；caught a glimpse 表示门口短暂的一瞥；coats 提供 rustle 的声音来源。
显示答案 · 本段词义
rustle：发出窸窣声；whisper：低语；耳语；foyer：门厅；休息厅；solitary：独自的；孤单的；silhouette：剪影；轮廓；glimpse：一瞥；短暂看见；retrieve：取回；找回；queue：队列

'Same old place,' her friend said. There was nostalgia in his voice. Mrs Zhou asked whether he had remembered the sweets. He had. Lin returned to the microphone, scarcely noticing that his anticipation had eased. During a brief consultation with Mei, he spotted one omission from his transcript: an invitation for questions. He wrote it at the top. The rest of the welcome could stay short; there was no need for improvisation.

🟣 表达拆解
He had 省略了 remembered the sweets；scarcely noticing... 表示本人几乎没有察觉；冒号具体说明 omission 是漏了哪一项。
🧠 段落回忆：临近开场，林补进讲稿的是什么？
提示1 · 逻辑链

提示2 · 猜词线索
Same old place 与 nostalgia 呼应；wrote it at the top 说明他在补上先前遗漏的内容；transcript 与 welcome 指向开场文字稿。
显示答案 · 本段词义
omission：遗漏；省略；improvisation：即兴发挥；transcript：文字记录；文字稿；anticipation：期待；预期；nostalgia：怀旧；对往事的怀念；scarcely：几乎不；勉强；consultation：协商；咨询

A hush settled over the room. Lin glanced at his notes, then looked up. 'Good afternoon. Before we begin, is everyone comfortable?' Someone asked where to return the headphones. He answered and waited for any further questions. Mrs Zhou was opening a packet of sweets. When Mei dimmed the lights, he took the cue and sat down among the audience.

🟣 表达拆解
settled over... 表示安静逐渐笼罩整个房间；where to return... 是疑问词加不定式；took the cue 指从灯光变化领会到该入座的信号。
🧠 段落回忆：实际开场时，林说了什么、做了什么？故事最后停在哪里？
提示1 · 逻辑链

提示2 · 猜词线索
