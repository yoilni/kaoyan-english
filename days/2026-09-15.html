Reading A · The Puzzle
为什么制造业增长与居民消费疲弱可以同时存在？

China's August data do not show weakness everywhere. Industrial output grew 5.2% from a year earlier, while retail sales rose only 0.4%. The gap reveals an economic imbalance: factories can remain buoyant, or relatively strong, even while household demand is sluggish and grows only slowly. This divergence between production and consumption is the puzzle behind the headline numbers.

🟣 A1 表达拆解

while 在这里表示对比“而/然而”，不是“当……时”；or relatively strong 是对 buoyant 的同义解释。

🧠 A1 段落回忆

主旨：工业较强、消费较弱说明了什么？

工业较强 ↔ 消费较弱；先抓住“两个方向不同”。
想想“不平衡”“分化”，以及 strong / grows slowly 对两个形容词的解释。
imbalance＝失衡；buoyant＝活跃/强劲；sluggish＝低迷/迟缓；divergence＝分化/背离。

The difference matters because production can outpace household income for some time: factories may grow faster than the purchasing power of their customers. The two trends can even appear to decouple, moving less closely together. Yet they cannot remain separate forever. Rebalancing means restoring a healthier relationship in which stronger production is matched by stronger household demand.

🟣 A2 表达拆解

outpace A＝增长/发展快于A；appear to＝看起来似乎；in which 引出“更健康关系”的具体内容。

🧠 A2 段落回忆

主旨：为什么生产不能长期跑在居民需求前面？

生产增长更快 → 与收入走势越来越不同 → 最终需要重新匹配。
冒号后解释 outpace；moving less closely together 解释 decouple；最后一句直接定义 rebalancing。
outpace＝超过/增长更快；decouple＝脱钩；rebalancing＝再平衡。

One reason households hold back is uncertainty. When people worry about future jobs or property values, they save more to protect themselves against possible trouble. This precautionary saving reduces current purchases. If caution persists, consumption may languish, remaining weak for a long time, and a once-active property market may become stagnant, with little growth or movement.

🟣 A3 表达拆解

hold back＝克制/减少支出；if caution persists＝如果谨慎情绪持续；两个逗号后的成分都在补充解释词义。

🧠 A3 段落回忆

主旨：不确定性如何一步步压低消费？

担心未来 → 多储蓄 → 当前少买 → 消费长期弱、市场缺少增长。
protect themselves against trouble 对应“预防”；remaining weak for a long time 和 little growth 分别解释后两个词。
precautionary＝预防性的；languish＝长期低迷；stagnant＝停滞的。

Weak demand creates a second problem. If factories can make far more goods than customers want, overcapacity emerges. Machines then sit idle or operate below their potential and become underutilized. Companies may respond by cutting prices. When such price pressure spreads through the economy, it becomes deflationary, making falling prices another obstacle to profits and investment.

🟣 A4 表达拆解

far more ... than ...＝远多于；respond by doing＝通过做某事来应对；making... 表示前面结果进一步造成的影响。

🧠 A4 段落回忆

主旨：需求追不上产能后为什么会出现价格压力？

生产能力 > 购买需求 → 设备闲置 → 企业降价 → 整体价格承压。
far more goods than customers want、below their potential、falling prices 分别给三个词提供定义线索。
overcapacity＝产能过剩；underutilized＝未充分利用；deflationary＝通缩性的。

Firms also change how they manage inventories. When sales disappoint, they may start destocking by reducing goods already held in warehouses. Suppliers then delay replenishment, the process of refilling those inventories. What begins as weak consumer demand therefore becomes a headwind—a force making growth harder—and may create a bottleneck that prevents factories from turning capacity into profitable sales.

🟣 A5 表达拆解

What begins as A becomes B＝起初只是A，后来演变成B；prevent A from doing B＝阻止A做B。

🧠 A5 段落回忆

主旨：销售疲弱怎样沿库存链传到企业？

卖不动 → 减库存 → 供应商少补货 → 增长受阻、产能难变成销售。
by reducing...、the process of refilling...、a force making growth harder 都是直接释义；最后看 prevents...from... 推 bottleneck。
destocking＝去库存；replenishment＝补库存；headwind＝阻力；bottleneck＝瓶颈。

Strong output also does not guarantee strong employment. Modern plants often rely on machinery and expensive equipment, making them capital-intensive. Other businesses require many workers and are more labor-intensive. As industry becomes more automated, production may rise without a similar increase in payroll, the total amount paid to workers, or in each new job vacancy that employers need to fill.

🟣 A6 表达拆解

does not guarantee＝并不保证；without a similar increase in...＝却没有……的相应增长，是考研常见反预期结构。

🧠 A6 段落回忆

主旨：为什么产出增长不等于就业同步增长？

机器资本多 ↔ 工人多；自动化提高产出，但工资支出和空缺岗位未必同步增加。
machinery/equipment 与 many workers 构成对比；后半句直接解释 payroll 和 vacancy。
capital-intensive＝资本密集型；labor-intensive＝劳动密集型；payroll＝工资总额；vacancy＝职位空缺。

That gap can be painful for workers. A factory layoff removes jobs, but even people who remain employed may face underemployment if they can find only fewer hours or less suitable work than they want. If opportunities disappear altogether, joblessness rises. The result is subdued household spending: consumption stays below normal because employment income and confidence are both weaker.

🟣 A7 表达拆解

even people who remain employed 强调“有工作也可能就业不足”；The result is... 明确给出整条就业链的结果。

🧠 A7 段落回忆

主旨：就业质量下降怎样影响消费？

岗位被削减/工时不足 → 收入和信心弱 → 消费低于正常水平。
removes jobs、fewer hours or less suitable work、opportunities disappear、stays below normal 都紧跟目标词提供线索。
layoff＝裁员；underemployment＝就业不足；joblessness＝失业状态；subdued＝受抑制的/疲弱的。

Debt reinforces the same cycle. An indebted family may use extra income to repay loans rather than shop; in doing so it tries to deleverage, or reduce its debt burden. Property developers facing weak sales may also enter retrenchment, cutting investment and costs. The damage can then produce a spillover: weakness that began in housing spreads into retail, services and the broader economy, creating a persistent malaise of low confidence and weak activity.

🟣 A8 表达拆解

rather than＝而不是；in doing so＝这样做时/由此；weakness that began in A spreads into B＝A中的疲弱向B扩散。

🧠 A8 段落回忆

主旨：债务如何把房地产疲弱传导到更广的经济？

负债 → 优先还债 → 企业收缩 → 房地产弱势扩散 → 更广泛低迷。
repay loans、reduce its debt burden、cutting investment and costs、spreads into...、low confidence and weak activity 分别解释五个词。
indebted＝负债的；deleverage＝去杠杆；retrenchment＝收缩；spillover＝外溢效应；malaise＝长期低迷。
Reading B · The Response
既然问题在于增长没有充分传导到家庭，政策应该怎样回应？
承接 Reading A：前文已经解释“生产强、家庭需求弱”的原因；Reading B 不重新建立背景，而是直接追问政策怎样修复这条传导链。

Fiscal policy starts with a simple question: what happens after the government releases money? A fiscal disbursement is the release of approved public funds. If recipients spend that money and others spend the resulting income again, the original payment creates additional activity. Economists call this extra effect the fiscal multiplier. The multiplier is smaller, however, when much of the new income is saved rather than spent.

🟣 B1 表达拆解

what happens after... 用问题引出机制；call A B＝把A称为B；saved rather than spent＝被储蓄而不是被消费。

🧠 B1 段落回忆

主旨：政府支出为什么可能产生超过原始金额的经济活动？

政府发钱 → 收款者消费 → 别人获得收入再消费 → 原始支出被放大。
is the release of... 直接定义第一个词；call this extra effect... 直接命名第二个词。
disbursement＝拨款/资金发放；multiplier＝乘数效应。

This explains why the destination of money matters. Redistribution shifts income between groups, for example toward households more likely to spend an extra dollar. Their disposable income—the money available after taxes—then supports consumption. Such policy may also reduce inequality when the gap between richer and poorer households is large. The aim is not simply to spend more, but to make each unit of support reach demand more effectively.

🟣 B2 表达拆解

more likely to do＝更可能做；not simply A, but B＝关键不只是A，而是B，注意这里在强调资金效率而非支出规模。

🧠 B2 段落回忆

主旨：为什么“钱给谁”会影响财政刺激效果？

收入转向更愿消费的家庭 → 税后可用收入增加 → 消费更强，也可能缩小贫富差距。
shifts income between groups、money available after taxes、gap between richer and poorer households 是三个直接释义。
redistribution＝再分配；disposable＝可支配的；inequality＝不平等。

The opposite approach can intensify weakness. When a government sharply cuts spending to control debt, the policy is known as austerity. During a downturn, such cuts may remove demand precisely when households are already cautious. This can deepen pessimism: if families and firms increasingly expect conditions to worsen, they may postpone purchases and investment, making the original slowdown harder to reverse.

🟣 B3 表达拆解

precisely when＝恰恰在……的时候；making ... harder to reverse＝使……更难逆转，表示前面行为的进一步后果。

🧠 B3 段落回忆

主旨：经济疲弱时削减政府支出为什么可能形成恶性循环？

经济弱 → 政府削支出 → 需求更弱 → 预期恶化 → 消费投资继续推迟。
cuts spending to control debt 定义 austerity；expect conditions to worsen 定义 pessimism。
austerity＝财政紧缩；pessimism＝悲观情绪。

Policy must also help workers move toward new opportunities. A growing service sector can improve the economy's absorption of workers displaced from old industries by taking more of them into new jobs. Entrepreneurship—the creation and development of new businesses—can widen that path. The goal is to protect people's livelihood, their means of earning enough to live, rather than preserving every existing job forever.

🟣 B4 表达拆解

workers displaced from...＝从……中被替代/挤出的劳动者；rather than preserving... 表明政策目标是保护生计，不是冻结旧岗位。

🧠 B4 段落回忆

主旨：产业变化后怎样让失去旧岗位的人重新进入经济循环？

旧产业释放劳动者 → 新行业吸纳 → 新企业创造机会 → 保护收入来源。
taking more of them into new jobs、creation and development of new businesses、means of earning enough to live 都是释义。
absorption＝吸收/消化能力；entrepreneurship＝创业；livelihood＝生计。

Better health and retirement protection can reduce the precaution families feel they must take against future risks. They may then hold fewer excessive savings. This does not mean saving is bad; it means stronger security can reduce the need to save unusually large amounts out of fear.

🟣 B5 表达拆解

This does not mean A; it means B 是作者限定观点的常见结构：不是反对储蓄，而是减少因恐惧造成的过度储蓄。

🧠 B5 段落回忆

主旨：社会保障为什么可能间接促进消费？

退休/医疗保障更可靠 → 对未来风险担忧下降 → 不必过度存钱 → 当前消费空间增加。
income after retirement、take against future risks、save unusually large amounts 分别给词义线索。
pension＝养老金；precaution＝防范；savings＝储蓄。

But if millions of families cut consumption at the same time, businesses earn less and total demand falls. Individual caution can therefore create a collective problem. Consumer sentiment—the overall mood and confidence of households—matters because fear can spread the same behavior across many families at once.

🟣 B6 表达拆解

at the same time 在这里是“同时发生”造成总量效应；individual ... collective ... 是“个体合理—整体有害”的核心对比。

🧠 B6 段落回忆

主旨：为什么个人合理的节俭可能变成整体经济问题？

单个家庭少花钱合理 → 大家同时少花 → 企业收入和总需求下降；共同情绪会放大行为。
careful spending and saving 定义 thrift；overall mood and confidence 直接定义 sentiment。
thrift＝节俭；sentiment＝情绪/信心。

Long-term change adds another challenge. Industrialization, the expansion of industry across an economy, can raise productivity and incomes, while urbanization moves more people and economic activity toward cities. But a demographic shift toward fewer young workers and more older people changes what households need and how much they save. Growth policy must therefore adapt as the population and the structure of the economy change together.

🟣 B7 表达拆解

🧠 B7 段落回忆

主旨：为什么长期增长政策不能只看工业产出？

产业扩张、人口进城与年龄结构变化同时发生 → 家庭需求和储蓄方式也改变。
expansion of industry、toward cities、fewer young workers and more older people 分别给三个词建立具体画面。

Successful rebalancing closes that gap: productive strength and household prosperity reinforce each other instead of moving apart.

🟣 B8 表达拆解

🧠 B8 段落回忆

主旨：作者最终怎样定义真正的“再平衡”？

生产率、工资、生活水平一起改善＝靠拢；生产能力远超购买力＝错位；再平衡就是缩小差距。
