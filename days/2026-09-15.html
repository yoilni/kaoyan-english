Factories Run Faster, Households Hold Back: The Puzzle of Rebalancing Growth

China's August data show an economic imbalance, not simply overall weakness. Reuters reported that industrial output grew 5.2% from a year earlier, while retail sales rose only 0.4%. This divergence matters: a buoyant factory sector can support production even when household demand remains sluggish. Growth may therefore look while feeling subdued to consumers.

🟣 P1 表达拆解

not simply overall weakness：不是简单的“整体疲弱”。这里特意不用原版 a simple story of weakness，因为 story 虽可表示“解释/叙事”，但会给当前理解增加不必要负担。

while：这里表示对比，可理解为“但/然而”，不是“当……时候”。
in aggregate：从总体、整体数据来看。

🧠 P1 段落回忆

先只回答主旨：为什么经济数据不能简单概括成“整体都很弱”？

工业较强，但消费较弱。
找出“不平衡、分化、活跃、低迷、受抑制”的目标词。
答案：imbalance＝不平衡；divergence＝分化；buoyant＝活跃/有韧性的；sluggish＝低迷的；subdued＝受抑制的。

The contrast helps explain why rebalancing is difficult. Manufacturing can expand when capital and policy support , but consumption depends more directly on income expectations and a sense of security. When confidence is weak, households may adopt precautionary saving and allow spending to languish. A stagnant property market can deepen this caution because housing has long been an important form of household wealth.

🟣 P2 表达拆解

depends more directly on＝更直接地取决于……；a sense of security＝安全感。整段抓一条因果链即可：信心弱 → 更愿意存钱 → 消费弱。

🧠 P2 段落回忆

主旨：为什么制造业扩张不一定带来消费同步增长？

家庭消费还取决于收入预期和安全感。
预防性储蓄 → 消费长期低迷；房地产停滞又加深谨慎。
precautionary＝预防性的；languish＝长期处于低迷；stagnant＝停滞的；rebalancing＝再平衡。

Investment-led growth creates another risk. If productive capacity expands faster than final demand, overcapacity can emerge and factories may become underutilized. Producers may cut prices, creating a deflationary headwind for profits. Firms may begin destocking, while suppliers replenishment. Decisions that seem sensible for each firm can therefore make a slowdown worse for the economy as a whole.

🟣 P3 表达拆解

for each firm / as a whole：单个企业层面 / 整体经济层面。末句是考研常见“个体理性≠整体最优”的逻辑。

🧠 P3 段落回忆

主旨：需求跟不上产能时会发生什么？

产能过剩 → 利用不足 → 降价 → 去库存 → 延迟补库存。
overcapacity＝产能过剩；underutilized＝未充分利用；deflationary＝通缩性的；headwind＝阻力；destocking＝去库存；replenishment＝补库存。

Technology adds another complication. Modern manufacturing is often capital-intensive rather than labor-intensive, so higher output does not automatically produce similar growth in payrolls or vacancies. Machines can improve efficiency, yet displaced workers may need time to find suitable jobs. Underemployment and joblessness can therefore exist alongside strong factory statistics.

🟣 P4 表达拆解

rather than＝而不是；does not automatically＝并不必然；exist alongside＝与……同时存在。注意作者是在反驳“产出增加必然带来就业增加”的直觉。

🧠 P4 段落回忆

主旨：为什么工厂产出强不等于就业强？

资本密集型生产可以在少增加劳动力的情况下提高产出。
capital-intensive＝资本密集型；labor-intensive＝劳动密集型；payroll＝工资总额；vacancy＝职位空缺；underemployment＝就业不足；joblessness＝失业状态。

Debt creates a further . An indebted household may choose to deleverage rather than spend extra income, while a developer facing weak sales may pursue retrenchment. The resulting spillover from property to retail can deepen economic malaise. Caution that is reasonable for one household can thus contribute to weak demand across the economy.

🟣 P5 表达拆解

transmission channel＝传导渠道；rather than spend＝选择去杠杆而不是把额外收入花掉；contribute to＝促成/加剧。

🧠 P5 段落回忆

主旨：债务怎样把房地产问题传到消费？

负债 → 优先还债和收缩 → 减少支出 → 房地产疲弱外溢到零售。
indebted＝负债的；deleverage＝去杠杆；retrenchment＝收缩；spillover＝外溢效应；malaise＝低迷。

Policy therefore involves trade-offs. Fiscal disbursement can support activity, but its multiplier is weaker if recipients save much of the extra income. Austerity may worsen demand, whereas poorly targeted spending can waste resources. Redistribution may have a larger effect when money reaches households more likely to consume, although lasting reform must also address inequality and demographic pressure.

🟣 P6 表达拆解

trade-off＝权衡；whereas＝然而/相比之下；poorly targeted＝投放对象不精准的。重点不是“政府花钱有没有用”，而是“钱最终流向谁、被怎样使用”。

🧠 P6 段落回忆

主旨：为什么同样的财政支出可能产生不同刺激效果？

新增收入被消费还是储蓄，会影响乘数。
disbursement＝拨款/支出；multiplier＝乘数；austerity＝紧缩；redistribution＝再分配；inequality＝不平等；demographic＝人口结构的。

There is also a of thrift. Saving is for one family, but if millions of households increase savings at the same time, total demand can weaken. Better pension coverage and predictable public services may reduce the precaution families feel they need. The aim is not to discourage saving, but to reduce the uncertainty that makes unusually high saving seem necessary.

🟣 P7 表达拆解

paradox of thrift＝“节俭悖论”：个人多储蓄可能合理，但大家同时减少消费会压低总需求。The aim is not to A, but to B＝目的不是A，而是B。

🧠 P7 段落回忆

主旨：为什么个人合理的节俭可能造成整体需求不足？

所有家庭同时多存钱，就意味着整体消费减少。
thrift＝节俭；pension＝养老金；precaution＝防范。

The broader lesson concerns how growth reaches households. Strong manufacturing remains valuable, but sustainable convergence requires productivity gains to raise wages, employment and confidence as well as output. Policymakers must distinguish temporary pessimism from structural weakness and avoid a misalignment in which production expands much faster than household income. Rebalancing succeeds when productive strength and living standards improve together.

🟣 P8 表达拆解

as well as＝不仅……也……；distinguish A from B＝区分A与B。结论不是“制造业太强”，而是生产增长需要更充分地传导到居民收入和生活水平。

🧠 P8 段落回忆

主旨：作者认为真正的再平衡是什么？

不是削弱制造业，而是让生产率和产出增长更充分地传导到工资、就业和信心。
convergence＝趋同；pessimism＝悲观情绪；misalignment＝错位/不匹配。
