Factories Run Faster, Households Hold Back: The Puzzle of Rebalancing Growth

新词已查看 0/50　复习词已查看 0/20

实验版：正文约考研单篇阅读长度，只承担核心目标词；学习重点改为“段落主旨 → 原句语境 → 推断词义”。每段读完先不要看翻译，先打开“🧠 段落回忆”。想不起来时逐级查看提示，最后再核对答案和中文翻译。

China's August data present an economic imbalance rather than a simple story of weakness. Reuters reported that industrial output grew 5.2% from a year earlier, while retail sales rose only 0.4%. This divergence matters: a buoyant factory sector can support production even when household demand remains sluggish. Growth may therefore look resilient in aggregate while feeling subdued to consumers.

🧠 P1 段落回忆（读完再打开）
先回忆主旨：为什么“工业增长”与“消费疲弱”可以同时存在？
工业较强 ↔ 消费较弱，核心是两个部门的“分化/失衡”。
找出表示“不平衡、分化、低迷、受抑制”的词。
答案：imbalance＝不平衡；divergence＝分化；sluggish＝低迷的；subdued＝受抑制的；buoyant＝活跃而有韧性的。

The contrast helps explain why rebalancing is difficult. Manufacturing can expand when capital and policy support converge, but consumption depends on income expectations and security. When confidence is weak, households may adopt precautionary saving and allow spending to languish. A stagnant property market can deepen that caution because housing has long served as a store of wealth.

🧠 P2 段落回忆
先回忆主旨：为什么制造业扩张不一定带来消费同步增长？
信心不足 → 防范未来风险 → 多储蓄 → 少消费。
“预防性的”“长期低迷”“停滞的”分别对应哪些词？
答案：precautionary＝预防性的；languish＝长期处于低迷；stagnant＝停滞的；rebalancing＝再平衡。

Investment-led growth has another risk. If productive capacity expands faster than final demand, overcapacity can emerge and factories become underutilized. Producers may cut prices, creating a deflationary headwind for profits. Firms may begin destocking, while suppliers postpone replenishment. Individually sensible decisions can thus amplify a slowdown.

🧠 P3 段落回忆
先回忆主旨：需求跟不上产能时，企业会出现怎样的连锁反应？
产能过多 → 利用不足 → 降价压力 → 去库存 → 延迟补库存。
重点回忆 overcapacity、underutilized、deflationary、destocking、replenishment 在这条链上的位置。
答案：overcapacity＝产能过剩；underutilized＝未充分利用；deflationary＝通缩性的；headwind＝阻力；destocking＝去库存；replenishment＝补库存。

Technology complicates the picture because modern industry is often capital-intensive rather than labor-intensive. Higher output does not automatically produce proportional payroll growth or more vacancies. Mechanization can improve efficiency, yet displaced workers may need time to find suitable jobs. Underemployment and joblessness can therefore coexist with impressive factory statistics.

🧠 P4 段落回忆
先回忆主旨：为什么“工厂产出很好”不等于“就业很好”？
资本密集型产业提高产出，不一定按比例增加劳动需求。
资本密集型 ↔ 劳动密集型；工资总额；职位空缺；就业不足。
答案：capital-intensive＝资本密集型；labor-intensive＝劳动密集型；payroll＝工资总额；vacancy＝职位空缺；underemployment＝就业不足；joblessness＝失业状态。

Debt creates a further transmission channel. An indebted household may choose to deleverage rather than spend extra income, while a developer facing weak sales may pursue retrenchment. The resulting spillover from property to retail can deepen economic malaise. What looks like caution at the household level can become weak demand at the national level.

🧠 P5 段落回忆
先回忆主旨：债务如何把房地产问题传导到消费？
负债 → 优先还债/缩表 → 减少支出 → 房地产疲弱外溢到零售。
负债的、去杠杆、收缩、外溢效应、低迷。
答案：indebted；deleverage；retrenchment；spillover；malaise。

Policy therefore involves trade-offs. Fiscal disbursement can support activity, but its multiplier is weaker if recipients save much of the additional income. Austerity may worsen demand, whereas poorly targeted spending can waste resources. Redistribution may have a larger effect when money reaches households more likely to consume, although durable reform must also address inequality and demographic pressure.

🧠 P6 段落回忆
先回忆主旨：为什么“政府花更多钱”并不必然产生同样大的刺激效果？
钱到谁手里、被消费还是储蓄，会改变乘数效果。
拨款、乘数、紧缩、再分配、不平等、人口结构。
答案：disbursement；multiplier；austerity；redistribution；inequality；demographic。

There is also a paradox of thrift. Saving is prudent for one family, but if millions of households simultaneously increase savings during a slowdown, total demand can weaken. Better pension coverage and predictable public services may reduce the precaution families feel they need. The point is not to discourage saving, but to reduce the uncertainty that makes excessive saving rational.

🧠 P7 段落回忆
先回忆主旨：为什么个人理性的节俭可能造成整体需求不足？
一个人多存钱没问题；所有人同时多存钱，会让总消费下降。
节俭、养老金、防范。
答案：thrift＝节俭；pension＝养老金；precaution＝防范。

The broader lesson is about transmission rather than a contest between factories and consumers. Strong manufacturing remains valuable, but sustainable convergence requires productivity gains to reach wages, jobs and household confidence. Policymakers must distinguish temporary pessimism from structural weakness and avoid a misalignment in which production incentives grow faster than household income. Rebalancing succeeds when productive strength and living standards reinforce each other.

🧠 P8 段落回忆
先回忆主旨：作者最终认为真正的“再平衡”是什么？
不是削弱制造业，而是让生产率提升更有效传导到收入、就业和信心。
趋同、悲观、错位。
答案：convergence＝趋同；pessimism＝悲观情绪；misalignment＝错位/不匹配。
