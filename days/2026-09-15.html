Factories Run Faster, Households Hold Back

China's August data do not show weakness everywhere. Industrial output grew 5.2% from a year earlier, while retail sales rose only 0.4%. The gap between a stronger factory sector and weaker consumer spending reveals an economic imbalance. Because the two parts of the economy are moving in different directions, this divergence matters for future growth.

主旨：为什么不能简单说“经济整体疲弱”？

工业较强、消费较弱 → 两部分不一致。
imbalance＝不平衡；divergence＝分化/背离。

Manufacturing can expand when investment and policy support are strong, but household spending depends on confidence about future income. When people worry about jobs or property values, they often save more to protect themselves against future risk. This kind of precautionary saving leaves less money for current purchases. If that caution lasts, consumption may languish, remaining weak for a long period.

🧠 P2 回忆

主旨：不确定性为什么会压低消费？

担心未来 → 为风险多存钱 → 当前少消费 → 消费长期偏弱。
precautionary＝预防性的；languish＝长期处于低迷。

Investment can also create too much productive capacity. When factories are able to make far more goods than customers want to buy, overcapacity emerges. Some machines and production lines then sit idle or operate below their potential, leaving them underutilized. Companies may cut prices to attract buyers, adding downward pressure to prices across the economy.

🧠 P3 回忆

主旨：产能增长快于需求会发生什么？

能生产的太多 → 卖不完 → 设备利用不足。
overcapacity＝产能过剩；underutilized＝未充分利用的。

Strong factory output does not necessarily mean strong employment. Modern plants often rely heavily on machinery and expensive equipment rather than large numbers of workers. Such production is capital-intensive. By contrast, businesses that require many workers for each unit of output are labor-intensive. An economy can therefore produce more goods without creating jobs at the same speed.

🧠 P4 回忆

主旨：为什么产出增加不一定带来同等就业增长？

依赖机器和资本 ↔ 依赖大量劳动力。
capital-intensive＝资本密集型的；labor-intensive＝劳动密集型的。

Debt can weaken consumption through another chain. A heavily indebted household may use extra income to repay debt instead of shopping. In other words, it tries to deleverage by reducing the amount of debt relative to its finances. When many households behave this way at once, weaker spending can spread from the property market into shops and services.

🧠 P5 回忆

主旨：负债怎样传导为消费不足？

债务高 → 新收入优先还债 → 债务下降 → 消费减少。
indebted＝负债的；deleverage＝去杠杆。

Fiscal policy works through a similar question: what happens after the government spends money? Suppose one dollar of public spending leads businesses and households to spend again, creating more than one dollar of total activity. Economists call this additional effect the fiscal multiplier. But the effect is smaller when recipients save most of the money. One response is redistribution: shifting more income toward households that are more likely to spend it. The key point is therefore not merely how much government spends, but where the money goes.

🧠 P6 回忆

主旨：为什么同样一笔政府支出可能产生不同效果？

政府花钱 → 收入增加 → 收入再次被消费 → 形成额外经济活动。
如果钱到了更愿意消费的人手中，刺激效果可能更强；这涉及收入在群体之间重新分配。
multiplier＝乘数效应；redistribution＝再分配。注意：这两个词都能由前文定义反推，而不是靠硬背。

Saving creates a similar difference between individual and collective behavior. For one family, thrift—careful spending and saving—can be sensible. Yet if millions of families cut spending at the same time, businesses receive less income and total demand falls. This apparent contradiction is why economists speak of the “paradox of thrift.” Better pension protection may reduce the need for unusually high saving.

🧠 P7 回忆

主旨：为什么个人合理的节俭可能造成整体需求不足？

词后面的破折号已经给出定义：careful spending and saving。
thrift＝节俭。这里训练“同位解释/破折号定义”这种考研常见猜词线索。

The broader challenge is to make production gains reach ordinary households. When productivity, wages and consumption gradually move toward a more balanced relationship, there is greater convergence between industrial strength and living standards. If production keeps expanding while household income falls behind, the economy instead develops a misalignment between what it can produce and what its people can afford to buy. Rebalancing means narrowing that gap, not weakening manufacturing.

🧠 P8 回忆

主旨：作者认为真正的再平衡是什么？

生产、工资、消费逐渐靠拢＝一种关系；生产增长而收入掉队＝相反关系。
convergence＝趋同；misalignment＝错位/不匹配。
