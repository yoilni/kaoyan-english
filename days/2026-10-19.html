Does Changing Direction Waste the Years Before?

改变方向，过去的努力就白费了吗？

单一主题 · 706词 / 10段 / 50个新词 + 20个复习词

新词已查看 0/50　复习词已查看 0/20

先读清每段在回答什么，再猜词。想起一次你犹豫是否继续的经历：拦住你的，是目标仍值得，还是不愿承认自己已经改变？长按单词切换绿色掌握状态。

The registration page for graduate study is open, but you keep returning to an unfinished form. Two years of dedication lie behind it. What began as ambition has become a tedious routine of practice questions and late nights. You feel ambivalent about continuing, yet hardly dare confess it. To contemplate another path feels like betrayal. Must you renounce everything those years meant if you decide to go elsewhere?

🟣 表达拆解
What began as... has become... 对照最初的动机与现在的体验；To contemplate... 是主语；结尾 if 引出假设，并追问改变方向是否必然否定过去。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么一张尚未填完的报考表，会让人觉得换方向等于背叛？
提示1 · 逻辑链
从最初的志向，到重复的日常，再到不敢承认的犹豫。
提示2 · 猜词线索
practice questions and late nights 为 tedious 提供具体场景；ambivalent 对应想继续又想离开的两种感受。
显示答案 · 本段词义
ambition：抱负；志向；dedication：投入；献身精神；tedious：单调乏味的；contemplate：认真考虑；renounce：放弃；弃绝；ambivalent：心情矛盾的；态度不确定的；confess：坦白；承认

On campus, persistence is easy to commend. A peer who keeps working through disappointment seems resolute; we may deem someone who changes direction weak. We learn to equate loyalty to a goal with strength of character. Difficult work does require commitment. But we become complacent if we treat that lesson as the whole answer. A goal deserves another question: why choose it now?

🟣 表达拆解
deem someone weak 中 weak 是宾语补足语；equate A with B 表示把两者等同；does 强调作者承认坚持的价值，再用 But 补充其不足。
🧠 段落回忆：赞扬坚持没有错，但为什么还需要多问一个问题？
提示1 · 逻辑链
先承认投入的必要，再检查“坚持值得赞扬”能否独自决定目标。
提示2 · 猜词线索
keeps working through disappointment 解释 persistence、resolute；把目标与品格画等号，帮助理解 equate。
显示答案 · 本段词义
persistence：坚持；锲而不舍；resolute：坚定的；果断的；commend：赞扬；complacent：自满而不愿反思的；equate：将……等同；peer：同龄人；同辈；deem：认为；视为

Past sacrifice makes that question harder. You are prone to count the years already spent and speculate that one more will justify them. Continuing offers a consolation: the effort still seems to be leading somewhere. Yet the earlier years cannot be recovered. Ask whether the benefits of the next year outweigh its costs. You need not disregard the past to decide how to spend the remainder of your time at university.

🟣 表达拆解
one more 省略 year；justify them 中 them 指已经付出的那些年；Yet 将注意力从过去投入转向未来选择。
🧠 段落回忆：过去已经花了两年，为什么不能单独成为再花一年的理由？
提示1 · 逻辑链
区分无法收回的投入，与接下来仍能选择的时间。
提示2 · 猜词线索
benefits 与 costs 的比较提示 outweigh；already spent 与 remainder 对照过去和剩余部分。
显示答案 · 本段词义
sacrifice：牺牲；付出；remainder：剩余部分；prone：易于……的；outweigh：比……更重要；超过；speculate：推测；猜测；consolation：安慰；慰藉；disregard：忽视；不顾

But the plan has also become part of your identity. You fear losing credibility with people who remember your ambition. A blunt remark, "You always give up," turns a change into a stereotype about your character. A commonplace question about applications now sounds like an accusation. Your self-esteem follows an implicit rule: changing a plan means becoming unreliable. You feel remorse, although reconsidering a choice has not itself harmed anyone.

🟣 表达拆解
turn A into B 说明一次决定如何被扩大为性格标签；冒号把 implicit rule 的内容说清；although 区分内疚感与实际造成的伤害。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么重新考虑计划，会被体验成对自己品格的否定？
提示1 · 逻辑链
别人记住的志向→对改变的标签→把标签变成评价自己的规则。
提示2 · 猜词线索
You always give up 将一次变化概括成固定特征，解释 stereotype；没有伤人却像做错事一样，提示 remorse。
显示答案 · 本段词义
credibility：可信度；信誉；blunt：直言不讳的；生硬的；stereotype：刻板印象；commonplace：常见的；普通的；remorse：懊悔；悔恨；implicit：含蓄的；未明说的；self-esteem：自尊；自我价值感

Yet fear of judgment cannot tell you whether to stay. Neither can one weary evening. Rigorous study can be frustrating even when you value its purpose. Refrain from treating an impulse to escape as a final verdict. Instead, reassess the pattern: does difficulty hinder work you still care about, or does interest remain absent even on better days? A vague dislike becomes useful evidence only when you understand what causes it.

🟣 表达拆解
Neither can... 承接前句的否定；even when 表示承认目标仍可能受挫；does... or does... 明确区分两种原因。
🧠 段落回忆：怎么区分“事情很难”与“自己已经不再认同这个方向”？
提示1 · 逻辑链
排除他人评价与单次疲惫，再比较一段时间内困难和兴趣的关系。
提示2 · 猜词线索
impulse 与 final verdict 对照短暂冲动和最终结论；understand what causes it 反衬 vague 的不明确。
显示答案 · 本段词义
weary：疲惫的；rigorous：严格缜密的；hinder：妨碍；阻碍；vague：模糊的；不明确的；reassess：重新评估；impulse：冲动；推动力；refrain：克制；避免做

Try a modest, experimental change before making a permanent one. Suppose you help a laboratory explain its work to schoolchildren. You need a sound comprehension of the research, yet find yourself most absorbed in helping the visitors understand it. Afterwards, articulate what held your attention and why. These observations furnish evidence that introspection alone may miss. Science communication gains personal relevance: it involves the part of the work that held your attention.

🟣 表达拆解
before 限定先尝试、后决定的顺序；Suppose 引出假设场景；what held... 是 articulate 的宾语，要求把体验说具体。
🧠 段落回忆：一次小尝试，能提供单靠反复思考得不到的什么信息？
提示1 · 逻辑链
做一件具体的事→观察投入在哪一部分→让备选方向与实际体验相连。
提示2 · 猜词线索
modest 与 permanent 对照尝试的规模；observations 与 evidence 帮助推断 furnish 是提供，而非装备房间。
显示答案 · 本段词义
modest：适度的；不大的；experimental：试验性的；探索性的；comprehension：理解；理解力；relevance：相关性；切合程度；furnish：提供；articulate：清楚表达；introspection：自省；内省

Evidence does not remove practical limits. A course in another field may be incompatible with your timetable; learning new skills may necessitate another semester of study. Check which deadlines are binding and whether you are eligible for a different program. A sensible change may happen in stages. You cannot rectify every difficulty immediately; expecting that would place an undue burden on the decision. Constraints shape how you move, even when they do not decide what matters.

🟣 表达拆解
which... 与 whether... 并列作 Check 的宾语；expecting that 中 that 指立即解决所有困难；even when 区分行动方式与个人价值判断。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么认清想做什么之后，还要核实时间、资格等限制？
提示1 · 逻辑链
有兴趣不等于能立即转向；把方向判断与可行的行动安排连接起来。
提示2 · 猜词线索
与 timetable 冲突解释 incompatible；another semester 提示 necessitate 的必要性；every difficulty immediately 说明 undue 为什么过度。
显示答案 · 本段词义
incompatible：不相容的；不能并存的；necessitate：使成为必要；sensible：明智的；合乎情理的；rectify：纠正；改正；undue：过度的；不适当的；binding：有约束力的；必须遵守的；eligible：符合条件的；有资格的

If you do change direction, what can you salvage from the earlier endeavor? The answer is specific: reading habits, stronger writing, and questions you can now explain. These abilities remain intact if you leave the research route. To depict those years as worthless would erase what they taught you. Education can encompass discovering work you do not want. The original destination becomes secondary; the lessons you extract from the journey remain available.

🟣 表达拆解
If you do change direction 中 do 强调确实作出改变；To depict... 是句子主语；分号两侧对照目的地的变化与经验的保留。
🧠 段落回忆：不再抵达原来的目的地，为什么不等于过去毫无收获？
提示1 · 逻辑链
把没有实现的原目标，与仍能保留、使用的具体能力分开。
提示2 · 猜词线索
reading habits、writing 等列举可保留的成果，解释 salvage、intact；worthless 与这些实际收获形成对照。
显示答案 · 本段词义
extract：提取；从……中获得；encompass：包含；涵盖；endeavor：努力；尝试；secondary：次要的；depict：描绘；描述；salvage：挽救；从……中保留下来；intact：完整无损的；保留完好的

For this reason, gratitude for past effort can exist alongside a decision to redefine your goal. You made the earlier choice with less knowledge than you have now. A more lenient judgment recognizes that difference. It is not contradictory to respect that choice and revise it. Your earlier effort helped you learn more about what you want. Compassion here means allowing that knowledge to change what you do next.

🟣 表达拆解
For this reason 明确承接上一段仍有收获的理由；that difference 指前后掌握的信息不同；that knowledge 指过去的投入带来的新认识，最后一句据此解释 compassion。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么尊重过去的选择，与依据新认识改变它，并不冲突？
提示1 · 逻辑链
过去确有收获→现在知道更多→允许新认识改变行动。
提示2 · 猜词线索
less knowledge than... 是宽容评价的理由；respect 与 revise 可以并存，帮助推断 contradictory 的反义关系。
显示答案 · 本段词义
redefine：重新界定；lenient：宽容的；不严厉的；contradictory：相互矛盾的；alongside：与……一起；与……同时；gratitude：感激；感恩；compassion：同情；体谅

Back at the registration page, you still lack a definitive answer. You may solicit advice, arrange another conversation, or decide that continuing is, for now, satisfactory. The next step need not be dramatic to be courageous. A candid question may provoke a better decision than another demand to be strong: "Why would I choose this path for another year?" The answer may not be straightforward. Determination can mean accepting the work of finding it.

🟣 表达拆解
Back at... 回到开头场景；for now 保留日后修正的空间；finding it 中 it 指值得选择下一年的答案。
🧠 段落回忆：最后的问题，怎样把“证明过去没错”转变成对未来的选择？
提示1 · 逻辑链
回到表格→保留继续或改变的可能→把注意力放到下一年的理由上。
提示2 · 猜词线索
