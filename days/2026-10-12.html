Why Leaves Change Color in Autumn

秋天的树叶为什么变色？

单一主题 · 702词 / 10段 / 50个新词 + 20个复习词

新词已查看 0/50　复习词已查看 0/20

沿着绿色消退、其他颜色出现与落叶前的准备读下去。先猜词、回忆主旨，再展开提示和翻译；长按单词切换绿色掌握状态。

On an autumn walk, you notice foliage changing along the path. One tree has a yellow hue; another is turning crimson, a deep red. These are deciduous trees, which usually shed their leaves each year. Their seasonal change may become perceptible over several days. Look closely and you can discern differences even between neighboring branches. Why does green disappear, and why does it leave different colors behind?

which 引导补充说明；even between... 强调差异可以发生在很小的范围内。

Start with pigment, a substance that gives something its color. The green pigment chlorophyll captures light energy. It is indispensable to photosynthesis, the process that uses light to make food. This allows plants to synthesize sugars from water and carbon dioxide. A sugar is a carbohydrate, a type of energy-rich compound. Green is therefore an integral part of a leaf's summer work, rather than mere decoration.

🟣 表达拆解

提示2 · 猜词线索

显示答案 · 本段词义

As autumn advances, daylight hours become shorter. Falling temperatures can also hamper the chemical reactions within a leaf. These metabolic activities are the processes that keep cells working. Their pace is contingent on conditions, not simply the date. Fresh supplies of chlorophyll begin to dwindle as production slows. The tree prepares for a dormant winter state. It must conserve resources rather than maintain every leaf indefinitely.

🟣 表达拆解
As 引出季节推进；contingent on 表示取决于；rather than 解释保存资源对应的选择。

提示2 · 猜词线索

显示答案 · 本段词义

Existing chlorophyll undergoes degradation: its molecules are broken down. The suppression of fresh production means less green replaces what is lost. This can uncover yellow and orange pigments already present. A golden tint, or slight coloring, becomes visible. Some leaves take on an amber shade, between yellow and orange. Their luminous appearance comes from sunlight, not light they produce themselves. This exposure of hidden colors is a foreseeable result of losing green.

🟣 表达拆解
what is lost 是 replaces 的宾语；already present 限定这些色素并非此刻才出现。

Red often follows a different route. Glucose and other sugars can remain in leaves during autumn. Their accumulation can encourage production of a new red pigment, anthocyanin. This pigment is soluble, meaning it can dissolve in water within cells. Sunny days and cool nights can be conducive to vibrant reds. The display is transient, lasting only part of the season. Yellow may be revealed, while red can be newly made.

🟣 表达拆解
meaning 引出解释；while 对比黄色显现与红色生成两条路径。

Cool weather, however, is different from a severe frost. Freezing can damage leaves before rich colors develop. A shortage of moisture may also cause dehydration, or excessive water loss. Leaves can wilt, becoming limp as their water supply fails. Adequate precipitation helps prevent such stress. Both drought and early freezing can be detrimental to the display. They may jeopardize the color season by making leaves fall prematurely, rather than improving it.

🟣 表达拆解

Color change also accompanies the recovery of useful materials. Nitrogen is a nutrient that leaves need to function. The tree can retrieve some of it before the leaves fall. Other mineral resources can also be moved into lasting tissues. Even a fraction recovered remains reusable when spring growth begins. It is imperative to avoid losing everything with each leaf. Materials that helped nourish summer growth can support the next season too.

🟣 表达拆解

Eventually, changes at the base of the leaf stem prepare it to detach. A separation layer helps sever the connection to the branch. Wind can then carry the leaf away. Meanwhile, the tree forms a protective seal over the exposed site. This makes it harder for harmful organisms to penetrate. Without protection, exposed tissues would be more susceptible to damage. Leaf fall is thus an organized process, not just a leaf being torn off.

🟣 表达拆解

This sequence is shaped by climatic conditions around each tree. Latitude influences the seasonal pattern of daylight. Altitude can bring cooler conditions than nearby low ground. Species also differ, so the timing is variable. Such variability produces a mosaic of colors across a hillside. A local deviation from an expected date need not indicate disease. Autumn color is the combined result of leaf chemistry and the conditions that alter its pace.

🟣 表达拆解

Return to one specimen, a single tree you can revisit. Repeated observations reveal the chronology of its changes. A mottled leaf may carry green and yellow patches together. Another may show a gradient, a gradual shift between colors. One plausible explanation is uneven pigment loss, but it deserves scrutiny rather than certainty from a photograph. The picturesque scene is worth enjoying. Its beauty becomes more interesting when you can trace the process behind it.
