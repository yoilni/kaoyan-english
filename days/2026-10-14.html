Why Exchange Rates Move Before Shop Prices

汇率变了，商店价格为什么没有马上变？

单一主题 · 703词 / 10段 / 50个新词 + 20个复习词

新词已查看 0/50　复习词已查看 0/20

沿采购、付款与定价这条链读下去。优先学习核心词的语境义；先猜词与回忆主旨，再展开提示和翻译。长按切换绿色掌握状态。

Imagine that your currency buys more dollars than it did yesterday. This appreciation suggests that imported goods should become cheaper. Yet a retailer keeps the same price on a camera. Your perception is that the shop has ignored the change. It is tempting to presume an instantaneous response to every fluctuation. But a transitory movement may not last. The question is how a currency change reaches an actual price tag.

🟣 表达拆解
It is tempting to... 表示很容易产生某种推断；how... reaches... 将表面现象转向传递过程。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么汇率已经变化，价签却可能暂时不变？
提示1 · 逻辑链
区分眼前的汇率变化与最终商品价格之间的环节。
提示2 · 猜词线索
instantaneous 与 yesterday / actual price tag 的时间差相连；presume 指尚未核实就作出假定。
显示答案 · 本段词义
fluctuation：波动；起伏；retailer：零售商；instantaneous：瞬间的；即时的；perception：看法；感知；presume：假定；推定；appreciation：升值；增值；transitory：暂时的；过渡性的

First examine the invoice, the supplier's request for payment. Its denomination tells you which currency the bill uses. A supplier may quote a fixed dollar amount. The arithmetic converts that amount into the buyer's currency. Appreciation changes this numerical result if the dollar price stays fixed. However, a fully proportional reduction in the final price would require more assumptions. Conversion explains only a partial link in the chain.

🟣 表达拆解
if 引出换算结果变化的条件；would require 表明后一结论需要额外前提，而非必然成立。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么采购账单的换算变化，不等于零售价同比例变化？
提示1 · 逻辑链
找出计算所固定的条件，再想最终售价是否只有这一项成本。
提示2 · 猜词线索
quote 在此表示报价；proportional 对应相同比例；partial 与 only 相连。
显示答案 · 本段词义
quote：报价；引用；arithmetic：算术；计算；numerical：数字的；数值的；proportional：成比例的；partial：部分的；不完全的；appreciation：升值；增值；invoice：发票；开票金额；denomination：计价币种；面额

The camera may already be in a warehouse. That cargo was purchased before today's currency movement. Freight costs were agreed earlier, and dispatch may have occurred weeks ago. Some bills remain payable, while later orders are still pending. Stock turnover determines how quickly old goods are replaced by new purchases. This does not force the shop to keep its price. It shows why today's exchange rate may differ from the cost recorded for existing stock.

🟣 表达拆解
while 对比旧账单与后续订单的不同状态；how quickly... 作 determines 的宾语，解释 turnover 的作用。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么查看今天的汇率，未必能知道货架上这台相机的采购成本？
提示1 · 逻辑链
回溯采购、运输与发货分别发生的时间。
提示2 · 猜词线索
dispatch 对应发货；pending 与 later orders still 未完成相连；turnover 对应旧货被新采购替代。
显示答案 · 本段词义
warehouse：仓库；cargo：货物；freight：货运；运费；dispatch：派遣；发送；发货；pending：待定的；尚未完成的；turnover：成交额；周转率；payable：应付款；应付的

Contracts add another delay. An agreement may stipulate a price that lasts until a stated expiry date. The counterparty has accepted those terms too. Keeping them can be compulsory under the agreement, leaving little discretion for immediate revision. Both sides may postpone discussion until renewal. An amendment can change the terms if they agree. The currency market moves continuously; their commercial agreement may change only at specific moments.

🟣 表达拆解
leaving... 表示前面约定带来的结果；if they agree 限定 amendment 改变条款的条件。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么市场每天变化，合同价格却可能过一段时间才调整？
提示1 · 逻辑链
比较持续交易的市场与按约定时点修改的合同。
提示2 · 猜词线索
stipulate 与 agreement 中的明确规定相连；expiry 对应到期日；compulsory 与 accepted terms 相连。
显示答案 · 本段词义
stipulate：规定；明确要求；compulsory：必须的；强制的；expiry：到期；失效；postpone：推迟；amendment：修订；修正；counterparty：交易对手方；discretion：自主决定权；酌情权

A firm's treasury department may also arrange a forward contract. It fixes an exchange rate for a future payment. The business is liable for the amount it has promised, and the payment remains obligatory. To settle the bill, it uses the agreed terms rather than today's market quote. Incidental daily movements then have less effect on this payment. The firm may withhold a price revision until its actual costs become clearer.

🟣 表达拆解
rather than 对比约定条款与当天报价；until 标记暂缓修改的时间边界。
🧠 段落回忆：约定未来付款汇率，怎样改变今日波动对企业成本的影响？
提示1 · 逻辑链
关注真正结算时使用的是哪一个汇率。
提示2 · 猜词线索
liable 和 obligatory 都与 promised payment 相连；settle 指结清；withhold 指暂缓作出或给出。
显示答案 · 本段词义
quote：报价；引用；obligatory：必须履行的；义务性的；withhold：暂不给予；保留；扣留；liable：负有责任的；可能遭受的；settle：结清；解决；安顿；incidental：偶然的；附带的；forward：远期的；远期合约；treasury：财资部门；国库

Next look beyond the foreign purchase. A merchant must pay personnel and lease local premises. It may undertake handling and delivery domestically, using local services. These expenses do not automatically fall when the currency strengthens. Translation of the foreign bill affects one part of the total. The residual cost remains. Even complete savings on the imported component would therefore produce a smaller percentage change in the final price.

🟣 表达拆解
Even... would... 表示即使满足前面条件，也仍有后面的限制；therefore 承接成本构成的解释。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么进口环节成本降了，最终售价仍可能只小幅变化？
提示1 · 逻辑链
比较进口成本在总售价中的比例与本地费用。
提示2 · 猜词线索
personnel 指人员；lease 是租用；residual 对应 foreign bill 之外仍保留的成本。
显示答案 · 本段词义
personnel：人员；员工；lease：租用；租约；merchant：商人；商家；domestically：在国内；在本国；undertake：承担；着手做；residual：剩余的；残余的；translation：折算；换算

The shop also wants to remain profitable. When costs rise, they squeeze its profit and compress the gap between costs and revenue. When costs fall, competition affects the response. Strong rivalry may encourage a shop to undercut another seller. Shoppers may resent unchanged prices when cheaper alternatives appear. Yet retail prices need not correspond exactly to a single cost. They reflect costs and the pressure to win customers.

🟣 表达拆解
When... / When... 对比成本上升与下降两种情形；need not 表示不一定，并非完全没有关联。
🧠 段落回忆：成本下降后，什么因素可能促使商店降价？
提示1 · 逻辑链
在成本变化之外，找出商店争取顾客时面临的压力。
提示2 · 猜词线索
undercut 与 another seller 的比较相连；resent 对应对不变价格的不满；correspond 指对应。
显示答案 · 本段词义
profitable：有利润的；有益的；undercut：以低价与……竞争；削弱；rivalry：竞争；对抗；resent：对……不满；怨恨；correspond：相对应；相一致；通信；squeeze：挤压；压缩；compress：压缩；挤压

That pressure differs across markets. If demand is inelastic, a lower price may attract relatively few additional buyers. The shop may have a weaker propensity to cut prices. Some prices consequently appear resistant to falling costs. Such inflexible behavior can produce asymmetry: increases and decreases need not pass through equally. The difference may be pronounced in some cases. Responsiveness depends on the market, so no single pattern describes every product.

🟣 表达拆解
If 引出假设条件；can / may 保留可能性；冒号解释 asymmetry，而不是断言所有市场均如此。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么同样的成本变化，在不同商品上可能表现得不同？
提示1 · 逻辑链
比较顾客对降价的反应与商店降低价格的动机。
提示2 · 猜词线索
inelastic 由 few additional buyers 解释；asymmetry 后给出定义；responsiveness 指反应程度。
显示答案 · 本段词义
inflexible：不灵活的；僵硬的；asymmetry：不对称；inelastic：缺乏弹性的；无弹性的；responsiveness：反应程度；响应性；resistant：抵抗的；不易受影响的；propensity：倾向；习性；pronounced：明显的；显著的

Evidence therefore needs to be granular, detailed enough to separate products. A preliminary estimate based on one shop can be misleading. A better comparison follows identical goods on successive dates. It asks whether an observable price change appeared after the purchase cost changed. A small effect may become discernible only over time. Other costs must be considered as well. The observed sequence helps test the explanation, without proving that currency movements caused every change.

🟣 表达拆解
whether 引出需要检验的问题；without proving... 为观察证据的解释范围保留边界。
🧠 段落回忆：怎样比较，才能更可靠地判断汇率与售价之间的关系？
提示1 · 逻辑链
固定商品、持续观察，并考虑其他成本也可能变化。
提示2 · 猜词线索
granular 用 detailed enough 解释；successive 对应连续日期；discernible 指可以看出。
显示答案 · 本段词义
preliminary：初步的；预备的；successive：连续的；相继的；observable：可观察的；identical：完全相同的；comparison：比较；对照；granular：细致的；颗粒化的；discernible：可辨别的；明显的

Return to the camera with an unchanged price tag. The result may seem counterintuitive because the intuitive calculation skips several stages. Financial literacy means noticing the distinct prices involved: market exchange rates, supplier bills, and final retail prices. A firm's pricing calculus connects them through time and costs. We should not generalize from one unchanged label. To reckon what changed, ask which bill, which date, and which part of the price.

🟣 表达拆解
because 解释为什么会违背直觉；which bill / which date / which part 三个并列问题总结判断路径。
