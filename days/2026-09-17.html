Reading A · From Faster Chips to Larger Systems

AI competition is no longer only a race to build the fastest chip. The architecture of an entire computing system now matters because thousands of processor units must cooperate. Their bandwidth, the amount of data they can move in a given time, and latency, the delay before data arrives, can determine whether expensive hardware actually delivers useful speed. This system view also changes how engineers think about scalable AI infrastructure.

🟣 A1 表达拆解

no longer only 表示竞争维度扩大；两个逗号后的结构直接解释 bandwidth 与 latency。

🧠 A1 段落回忆

主旨：为什么最快的单颗芯片不一定组成最快的系统？

大量处理器协作 → 数据移动速度和等待时间变重要 → 系统设计决定实际性能。
amount of data、delay before data arrives 是直接定义。
architecture 架构；processor 处理器；bandwidth 带宽；latency 延迟。

The key measure is increasingly throughput: how much useful work the whole system completes over time. A fast interconnect lets chips exchange data, while network topology describes how those components are arranged. Together they form a communication fabric linking processors, memory and storage. If that fabric is slow, adding more chips can merely enlarge a familiar bottleneck.

🟣 A2 表达拆解

how much...over time 是 throughput 的定义；while 用来区分连接本身与连接布局。

🧠 A2 段落回忆

主旨：为什么更多芯片可能无法按比例提高算力？

芯片增多 → 数据交换增多 → 网络结构跟不上 → 瓶颈扩大。
exchange data、how components are arranged、linking processors 给出语义线索。
throughput 吞吐量；interconnect 互连；topology 拓扑；fabric 互连网络；memory 内存。

Large models impose different demands during training, when parameters are learned, and inference, when a trained model answers users. Both consume compute, but not in identical ways. Designers therefore seek parallelism, splitting work across many devices, while controlling synchronization so that those devices remain coordinated. Otherwise communication overhead can consume time that should have been spent on calculation.

🟣 A3 表达拆解

when... 分别给 training/inference 下定义；otherwise 引出协同失败的结果。

🧠 A3 段落回忆

主旨：把任务分给更多设备为什么同时带来协调成本？

并行分工 → 必须同步 → 通信本身消耗时间。
splitting work 与 remain coordinated 分别解释两个核心词。
training 训练；inference 推理；compute 计算资源；parallelism 并行；synchronization 同步；overhead 额外开销。

This helps explain why AI hardware has become an ecosystem rather than a collection of isolated chips. Software compatibility matters because developers build tools around familiar platforms. A proprietary software stack may strengthen one supplier's position, while migration to a different platform requires code changes and testing. Those switching costs can slow adoption even when rival hardware looks attractive on paper.

🟣 A4 表达拆解

rather than 强调“系统生态而非孤立芯片”；最后一句解释为什么硬件参数不是唯一决策因素。

🧠 A4 段落回忆

主旨：软件生态如何形成硬件平台的黏性？

开发者依赖既有工具 → 换平台要改代码测试 → 转换成本降低采用速度。
code changes and testing 是 migration 成本的具体化。
ecosystem 生态系统；compatibility 兼容性；proprietary 专有的；migration 迁移；switching 转换；adoption 采用。

Hardware supply adds another layer. Advanced chips require complex fabrication at a semiconductor foundry. The yield—the share of usable chips produced from manufacturing—affects cost, while each silicon wafer must pass through many steps. Advanced packaging then combines components closely so data can move faster. A shortage at any stage can become a system-wide constraint.

🟣 A5 表达拆解

破折号后的 share of usable chips 是 yield 的定义；at any stage 表明瓶颈可能出现在产业链任何位置。

🧠 A5 段落回忆

主旨：为什么 AI 芯片供给不能只看芯片设计？

制造 → 良率 → 晶圆加工 → 封装；任一环节不足都会限制供给。
semiconductor、usable chips、silicon、combines components 是定位线索。
fabrication 制造；foundry 晶圆代工厂；yield 良率；wafer 晶圆；packaging 封装。

Data centers also need high-speed optical links to preserve connectivity across large clusters. A modular design lets operators add standardized blocks instead of rebuilding everything. Engineers then use a benchmark to compare systems under similar tasks and measure real performance. Such tests matter because impressive component specifications do not automatically translate into efficient system-level results.

🟣 A6 表达拆解

instead of 解释 modular 的实际优势；最后一句是“部件参数≠系统结果”的反预期关系。

🧠 A6 段落回忆

规模扩大 → 需要高速连接与模块扩容 → 用统一测试验证真实表现。

Reading B · Supply, Substitution and the Price of Scale

Huawei's latest announcements illustrate this system strategy. Reuters reported on September 17 that demand for the company's AI computing equipment in China exceeds current supply and that it is linking very large numbers of processors to compensate for limits in individual chips. Rapid iteration therefore matters: each design cycle feeds the next. A product roadmap signals what comes later, while an indigenous technology base can support domestic substitution when access to foreign equipment is restricted. The goal is greater self-sufficiency, although complete independence is difficult in a global chip chain.

🟣 B1 表达拆解

feeds the next 表示一代产品为下一代积累经验；最后一句限制了“自主”的程度。

🧠 B1 段落回忆

主旨：供应受限时，为什么技术路线图和快速迭代更重要？

外部获取受限 → 国内替代需求上升 → 连续迭代形成自己的技术基础。
each design cycle、what comes later、domestic 分别提示前三词。
iteration 迭代；roadmap 路线图；indigenous 本土的；substitution 替代；self-sufficiency 自给自足。

Restrictions can create scarcity, but scarcity does not affect every company equally. Firms with stronger purchasing power may secure components first, while smaller developers face longer lead-time before delivery. They may keep extra inventory as insurance, yet that ties up working capital. The result is a difficult trade-off: resilience against disruption costs money that could otherwise fund research.

🟣 B2 表达拆解

ties up 表示资金被占用；冒号后直接解释 trade-off 的两难。

🧠 B2 段落回忆

主旨：为什么多备库存既安全又昂贵？

稀缺 → 交货等待 → 增库存 → 占资金 → 安全性与研发投入权衡。
before delivery、extra...as insurance、working... 是语境线索。
scarcity 稀缺；lead-time 交付周期；inventory 库存；capital 资本；trade-off 权衡。

Scale is expensive for another reason: AI infrastructure requires enormous expenditure before revenue arrives. The cost of equipment may be spread through depreciation over its useful life, while software or other intangible assets may be treated through amortization. But accounting cannot remove the cash requirement. Operators still need financing, and a project must appear bankable enough for lenders or investors to support it.

🟣 B3 表达拆解

before revenue arrives 是基础设施融资压力的核心；cannot remove 强调会计处理不等于现金消失。

🧠 B3 段落回忆

主旨：为什么会计上分摊成本不能解决 AI 基建的融资问题？

先巨额投入 → 会计分摊 → 现金仍要先支付 → 必须融资且项目要可信。
spread...over useful life 与 intangible assets 区分两种分摊。
expenditure 支出；depreciation 折旧；amortization 摊销；financing 融资；bankable 可融资的。

Some AI infrastructure companies turn to convertible debt, which can later become shares under specified conditions. It can raise cash without issuing ordinary equity immediately, but future conversion may cause dilution, reducing existing shareholders' percentage ownership. Heavy borrowing also increases leverage. Investors therefore watch cashflow, the money entering and leaving the business, rather than focusing only on rapid expansion.

🟣 B4 表达拆解

which can later become shares 直接解释 convertible；rather than 强调投资者不能只看扩张。

🧠 B4 段落回忆

主旨：可转债怎样同时解决现金需求并制造新的财务风险？

借钱获得现金 → 未来可能转股 → 股权稀释；借款增加也提高杠杆。
become shares、percentage ownership、money entering and leaving 是直接定义。
convertible 可转换的；dilution 稀释；leverage 杠杆；cashflow 现金流。

The deeper lesson is that chip competition is becoming a problem of coordination. Companies must align processors, networks, software, factories, electricity and finance. A system can have abundant capacity yet poor efficiency if one layer fails. It can accelerate deployment only when technical and financial pieces mature together. This is why the geography of AI may increasingly reflect complete industrial systems rather than a single spectacular component.

🟣 B5 表达拆解

align A, B and C 是多环节协同；yet 提醒“容量大但效率差”的反差。

🧠 B5 段落回忆

主旨：AI 竞争最终为什么是系统协调能力的竞争？

硬件+网络+软件+制造+电力+融资 → 任一短板拖累整体 → 协同决定部署速度。
align 多个环节就是 coordination 的具体含义。
复习：coordination 协调；capacity 容量；efficiency 效率；deployment 部署。

That system perspective also changes risk management. A reliable platform needs diversification across suppliers and technologies, because excessive dependency on one source creates vulnerability. Firms may mobilize more resources to remove a technical hurdle, but investment can still lag behind demand. The most durable advantage may therefore come not from one breakthrough, but from institutions capable of repeated adaptation.

单一依赖带来脆弱性 → 分散供应 → 动员资源解决障碍 → 持续适应。
