Reading A · Why a cheap repair can still be costly
为什么维修费低，维修却未必划算？

Imagine a family whose washing machine stops halfway through a cycle. The appliance still looks new, but a malfunction has left its drum full of water. The parents search for help, then pause when they see a new machine advertised with next-day delivery. Their hesitation is understandable. Before deciding whether to repair anything, a household needs answers about cost, time, and the chance that the problem will return.

🟣 表达拆解
whose 引导定语从句，说明是哪一个家庭；has left its drum full of water 是 leave + 宾语 + 状态。Before deciding... 把具体情境推进到一般决策。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么这家人还没得到维修报价，就开始考虑换新？
提示1 · 逻辑链
比较维修的不确定性与广告中明确承诺的送达时间。
提示2 · 猜词线索
malfunction 对应 stops halfway；hesitation 由 search for help, then pause 表现出来。
显示答案 · 本段词义
malfunction：故障；失灵；appliance：家用电器；器具；hesitation：犹豫；迟疑

A technician must first diagnose the fault: identify what has gone wrong, rather than simply replace whatever looks old. Suppose a small pump is defective while the motor is still sound. That distinction matters. The deterioration of one part does not mean the whole machine has reached the end of its useful life. Yet the family cannot make that judgment from a flashing warning light alone.

🟣 表达拆解
冒号解释 diagnose 的具体动作。Suppose 引入假设，while 对比水泵与电机的状态；does not mean 阻止读者从局部故障推到整体报废。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么确认故障位置会改变换新的判断？
提示1 · 逻辑链
先区分局部部件和整台机器，再考虑家庭缺少什么信息。
提示2 · 猜词线索
diagnose 后有 identify what has gone wrong；defective 与 still sound 形成反义对照。
显示答案 · 本段词义
technician：技术员；维修技师；diagnose：诊断；查明问题；defective：有缺陷的；有故障的；deterioration：恶化

Next comes the quotation, a statement of the expected price. Even an affordable repair can bring inconvenience: someone may need to stay home twice, first for an assessment and later for the work. If the machine is likely to fail again soon, repair may be uneconomical despite a low initial charge. The relevant comparison therefore includes time and expected service, not just the two prices on a screen.

🟣 表达拆解
first... and later... 说明 twice 的具体安排。If... 引出条件；despite 后接名词短语，与“收费低”形成让步关系。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么较低的维修报价不一定代表更划算？
提示1 · 逻辑链
把账面费用、等待时间与修好后还能用多久放在一起比较。
提示2 · 猜词线索
quotation 有同位解释；inconvenience 由两次留家说明；uneconomical 的线索是很快再次故障。
显示答案 · 本段词义
quotation：报价；引文；uneconomical：不经济的；不划算的；inconvenience：不便；麻烦；affordable：负担得起的

Price also reflects the way a product is built. A repairer may have to dismantle an outer case before reaching the pump. Strong adhesive that holds the case together can make this difficult; brittle plastic may crack instead of bending. A cheap part can thus require expensive labor to reach. The design decisions made in a factory shape the repair bill long before the owner notices a fault.

🟣 表达拆解
that holds... 修饰 adhesive；instead of bending 用动作对比解释材料表现。long before 把维修账单追溯到更早的设计阶段。
🧠 段落回忆：一个很便宜的零件，为什么可能对应很贵的维修？
提示1 · 逻辑链
区分零件本身的价格与接触零件所需的劳动。
提示2 · 猜词线索
dismantle 与 reaching the pump 联系；adhesive 对应 holds together；brittle 由 crack instead of bending 解释。
显示答案 · 本段词义
dismantle：拆卸；拆除；adhesive：黏合剂；有黏性的；brittle：易碎的；脆的

Access is only the first hurdle. A new control unit must be compatible with the machine's circuitry, the connected electrical paths that allow it to operate. A part that fits physically may still fail to work with the existing system. If the required unit has become obsolete and is no longer supplied, the search gets harder. These obstacles can create a strong disincentive to attempt repair at all.

🟣 表达拆解
A part that fits... 中 that 修饰 part；may still 表明外形适配并不足够。If 从句再增加一个条件，把技术问题推进到供货问题。
🧠 段落回忆：“装得进去”为什么不等于“能修好”？
提示1 · 逻辑链
区分外形、电气系统和市场供货三个层面。
提示2 · 猜词线索
compatible 由 work with 解释；circuitry 后有同位说明；obsolete 与 no longer supplied 呼应。
显示答案 · 本段词义
compatible：兼容的；能共存的；circuitry：电路；电路系统；obsolete：过时的；淘汰的；disincentive：抑制因素；使人不愿行动的因素

It would be unfair to call the family wasteful simply because they consider buying new. A frugal household tries to avoid unnecessary expense, while a conscientious buyer also thinks carefully about environmental harm. Neither wants to squander money on repeated failed repairs. Their reluctance may reflect a poor service system rather than indifference. Asking people to care more will achieve little if acting on that concern remains costly and uncertain.

🟣 表达拆解
It would be unfair to... 是评价句型；Neither 承接前面两类购买者。rather than 提供对同一行为的另一种解释。

提示1 · 逻辑链
想一想一个既节约又负责的人，是否仍可能合理地拒绝某次维修。
提示2 · 猜词线索
frugal 对应 avoid unnecessary expense；conscientious 对应 thinks carefully；squander 与 failed repairs 联系。
显示答案 · 本段词义

There is nevertheless a wider cost to replacing goods too soon. Making another machine requires materials, beginning with extraction from the earth. Continued use of limited resources contributes to their depletion, while the old product still needs proper disposal. Some of these costs are absent from the shop price. When repair safely adds useful years, the benefits can accrue beyond the household that pays the bill.

🟣 表达拆解
beginning with... 补充材料来源；while 连接新产品生产与旧产品处置两端。When 从句限定最后的收益判断。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么维修的收益可能不只归支付维修费的家庭？
提示1 · 逻辑链
把视线从商店价格延伸到生产前端和废弃后端。
提示2 · 猜词线索
extraction 对应 from the earth；depletion 对应 limited resources 被持续使用；disposal 对应旧产品的最终去向。
显示答案 · 本段词义

Waste policy must also consider what happens after collection. Sending electrical waste to landfill, where rubbish is buried, can lose useful materials and create risks when toxic substances escape into the environment. Preventing contamination requires safe handling, not merely a collection box. Repair can delay this stage, but it cannot remove the need for it forever. Reading A therefore leads to a practical question: how can longer use become a dependable choice?

🟣 表达拆解
where 引导定语从句，解释 landfill；when 限定风险产生的情形。not merely 和 but 分别限制“收集”与“维修”的作用。

提示1 · 逻辑链
区分推迟报废与永久消除报废，再看收集和安全处理的区别。
提示2 · 猜词线索

显示答案 · 本段词义

Reading B · Making repair a dependable choice
怎样让维修成为可靠的选择？

One response is to make repair a legal responsibility. The EU's repair directive required national rules to apply from 31 July 2026. Its binding duties concern specified goods covered by EU repairability requirements; being eligible for repair under these rules does not mean every repair is free. Nor should a manufacturer's commercial warranty be confused with rights provided by law. Clear information must tell the family which protection actually applies.

🟣 表达拆解
being eligible... 是动名词短语作主语。Nor should... 是否定副词前置引起的倒装；be confused with 表示“与……混淆”。
🧠 段落回忆：“享有维修权”为什么不能直接理解成“一切免费修”？
提示1 · 逻辑链
找出适用商品范围、费用与保障来源三个不同问题。
提示2 · 猜词线索
binding 与 duties 相连；eligible 指符合规定条件；commercial warranty 与 rights provided by law 对照。
显示答案 · 本段词义
binding：有约束力的；eligible：符合条件的；有资格的；warranty：保修；保证书

The rules also restrict unjustified obstruction of repair through contracts or hardware and software barriers. The economic concern is easy to see. If only one firm can obtain a necessary part or service manual, it may effectively monopolize repairs, leaving owners with little choice. Giving qualified independent repairers access can make competing offers possible. That does not eliminate safety requirements; it makes the reasons for restricting access worth examining.

🟣 表达拆解
If only... 提出假设，不是断言所有厂家都如此。leaving... 是结果状语；That 回指扩大合格维修者的获取渠道。

提示1 · 逻辑链

提示2 · 猜词线索

显示答案 · 本段词义

Better access works best when the product itself is designed for repair. A detachable panel can be removed without breaking the case, allowing a worker to replace the pump and reassemble the machine while leaving other parts intact. This is different from promising that every owner can safely repair everything. Good design can reduce the work required without pretending that skill and equipment are unnecessary.

🟣 表达拆解
allowing... 表示前述设计带来的结果；while leaving... 说明同时保持的状态。without pretending... 为最后的判断保留边界。

提示1 · 逻辑链

提示2 · 猜词线索

显示答案 · 本段词义

Trust matters as much as access. A competent repairer knows how to test the machine after the work; a meticulous one checks details that a rushed visit might miss. Customers cannot easily judge workmanship, the quality of that work, before using the machine again. A clear record of what was changed, what was tested, and what happens if the fault returns can reduce this uncertainty and support lasting restoration.

🟣 表达拆解
one 代替 repairer，避免重复。三个 what 从句并列，说明 record 应包含的信息；that a rushed visit might miss 修饰 details。

提示1 · 逻辑链

显示答案 · 本段词义

A larger repair market also needs people. Vocational education can teach practical skills, while an apprentice develops judgment by working alongside an experienced repairer. Such expertise takes time to build. A town cannot create it simply by announcing a subsidy for repairs. Training, access to technical information, and a steady flow of work need to develop together if local services are to survive beyond an initial campaign.

🟣 表达拆解

Support must also be easy to use. Imagine a local scheme that provides repair consultancy before a household chooses a service, and makes a small booking deposit refundable if no suitable appointment is available. Such details can reduce risk. By contrast, a cumbersome reimbursement process requiring repeated forms may discourage the very families it is meant to help. A useful scheme should be judged by completed repairs, not applications alone.

🟣 表达拆解

Sometimes the sensible outcome is not a return to the original owner. A workshop may refurbish a used machine for resale after a thorough inspection. If that is impractical, it may salvage usable parts while sending hazardous materials for specialist treatment. These options require honest descriptions of condition and careful checks. Extending a product's life should never mean passing an unknown safety problem to someone with less money.

Return to the family with wet clothes in the drum. They should be able to ask whether repair will prolong safe, useful service, what upkeep will be needed, and how the total cost compares with replacement. It is reasonable to remain skeptical of vague promises. The goal is neither to discard every broken product nor to preserve every old one. It is to make a well-informed choice possible before convenience makes the decision for us.
