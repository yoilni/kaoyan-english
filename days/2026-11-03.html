What a Successful Demonstration Cannot Tell Us

演示成功后，为什么还要做测试？

主题阅读 · 718词 / 10段 / 50个新词 + 20个复习词

新词已查看 0/50　复习词已查看 0/20

先抓住核心问题，再看每段怎样推进论证。长按单词可标记或取消绿色掌握状态。

A novice on a student robotics team watches a small vehicle carry a block across the lab. It passes every trial in the final rehearsal. Next morning, on a darker floor, its steering becomes erratic and the block ends up outside the marked area. The failure is easy to blame on a flaw in the code. But the earlier runs had already encouraged the team to assume too much.

🟣 表达拆解
ends up outside 表示实际结果；is easy to blame on 引出容易想到的解释；had already encouraged 把时间拉回故障发生之前。
🧠 段落回忆：问题出在第二天才发生，还是第一天已经埋下了判断上的隐患？
提示1 · 逻辑链
同一环境下多次成功→更换地面→表现改变→回看原先的判断。
提示2 · 猜词线索
方向不稳和方块出界提示 erratic；too much 提示 assume 的判断超出了已知事实。
显示答案 · 本段词义
trial：试验；一次试运行；erratic：不稳定的；无规律的；failure：失败；未达到预期；flaw：缺陷；瑕疵；assume：假定；想当然地认为；novice：新手；初学者；rehearsal：排练；试演

A demonstration can be satisfactory on its own terms: it shows that a design can do its job under familiar conditions. A rigorous test asks what would happen if we deliberately changed one of those conditions. If we omit that step, a neat run can be misleading. The distinction matters: we can conclude that the robot worked on the lab floor, but we do not yet know how it will perform elsewhere.

🟣 表达拆解
on its own terms 限定评价的范围；what would happen if 表示对改变条件后果的设问；yet 表明结论仍有待补充。
🧠 段落回忆：一次演示成功，究竟能支持多大范围的结论？
提示1 · 逻辑链
演示有明确范围→测试改变条件→观察结果→限定结论范围。
提示2 · 猜词线索
deliberately changed 与 omit that step 形成对照；worked on the lab floor / perform elsewhere 提示 distinction 所在。
显示答案 · 本段词义
deliberately：有意地；故意地；omit：省略；遗漏；misleading：容易使人误解的；distinction：区别；区分；conclude：得出结论；断定；satisfactory：令人满意的；合适的；rigorous：严格缜密的

No team can stage every hypothetical accident. It can ask which changes are plausible and which responses would be hazardous. Glare might cause distortion in the camera image. A replacement camera might use an incompatible format. A brief loss of the block in the image can be a precursor to an unforeseen turn. Trying these cases early gives the team a chance to mitigate the risks.

🟣 表达拆解
stage 在这里是“有意制造情境”；两个 which 从句分别问发生什么变化、变化会引起什么反应；give... a chance to 说明提前测试的作用。
🧠 段落回忆：情形很多、时间有限时，应根据什么选择测试条件？
提示1 · 逻辑链
无法穷尽情形→考察变化可能性和反应后果→选取有代表性的条件。
提示2 · 猜词线索
glare 影响图像，提示 distortion；短暂看不见方块发生在意外转向之前，提示 precursor。
显示答案 · 本段词义
hypothetical：假设的；设想中的；distortion：失真；扭曲；precursor：先兆；前身；unforeseen：未预料到的；mitigate：减轻；缓和；hazardous：危险的；有害的；incompatible：不相容的；不能并存的

Meticulous notes help the team tell whether a sudden stop came from a malfunction or a change in the setup. A sporadic fault may be hard to capture. Recording the light level and route allows others to check whether the stop is reproducible. They can then ascertain whether it also happens on another floor. Without such a record, the team cannot tell a pattern from a coincidence.

🟣 表达拆解
tell whether... or... 区分两种解释；then 表示先确认可重现，再改变条件；tell A from B 表示把两者辨别开。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么应当记录测试条件，而不是只记录“通过”或“失败”？
提示1 · 逻辑链
出现偶发停车→留下条件→复现原现象→比较换场地的结果。
提示2 · 猜词线索
hard to capture 提示 sporadic；pattern 的对照项提示 coincidence 的偶然性。
显示答案 · 本段词义
sporadic：偶发的；零星的；fault：故障；毛病；reproducible：可重现的；可复现的；ascertain：查明；确定；coincidence：巧合；偶然同时发生；meticulous：一丝不苟的；细致的；malfunction：故障；失灵

Even a repeatable result can invite an erroneous explanation. An inspection may show no damage to the camera, but it cannot tell us what the software received. To diagnose the problem, the team needs a hypothesis it can disprove. An independent instrument might show that the camera output is stable while the steering changes. That observation would warrant a closer look at the code.

🟣 表达拆解
invite 在这里指“引出”；it can disprove 是省略关系代词的定语从句；while 对照同一时刻的输入与转向。
🧠 段落回忆：现象可重现之后，怎样判断自己对原因的解释是否站得住？
提示1 · 逻辑链
同一现象可有多种原因→提出解释→取得区分证据→调整排查方向。
提示2 · 猜词线索
外观没坏却不能说明输入是什么，提示 erroneous；新观察带来进一步检查的理由，提示 warrant。
显示答案 · 本段词义
erroneous：错误的；不正确的；hypothesis：假设；待检验的解释；disprove：证明……不成立；驳倒；instrument：仪器；工具；warrant：使有正当理由；值得；inspection：检查；检验；diagnose：诊断；查明问题

Painstaking work to rectify one problem may introduce another. Increasing the camera's exposure can make the block easier to see while washing out the line the robot follows. The unintended effect can matter as much as the corrective change. A checklist can help the team retest earlier cases systematically. If the steering becomes inconsistent on surfaces it once handled well, the team has learned what the latest adjustment costs.

🟣 表达拆解
while washing out 写出同一调整的副作用；as much as 比较两件事的重要程度；what... costs 指改动带来的代价。
🧠 段落回忆：修好一处后，为什么还要重新测试以前已经通过的情况？
提示1 · 逻辑链
局部修正→另一功能受影响→用先前条件重测→判断改动的整体效果。
提示2 · 猜词线索
曝光帮忙看清方块，却冲淡了线，提示 unintended；曾经处理良好和现在时好时坏的对照提示 inconsistent。
显示答案 · 本段词义
unintended：非预期的；无意造成的；corrective：纠正的；用于补救的；checklist：核对清单；检查表；systematically：有条理地；系统地；inconsistent：不一致的；不稳定的；painstaking：极仔细的；煞费苦心的；rectify：纠正；改正

Before a run, the team should agree on its tolerance for error. A slight wobble may be permissible; delivering to the wrong area is unacceptable. An impartial observer can apply that rule more easily than a vague demand for perfection. When the camera loses the block, stopping may count as a pass. That case is a counterexample to the idea that the robot should always keep moving. Writing down the exception removes ambiguity about what should happen.

🟣 表达拆解
count as a pass 表示按事先约定可算通过；to the idea... 指出反例针对的观点；what should happen 是规则要明确的内容。
🧠 段落回忆：怎样确定小车“通过”测试，而不靠事后印象？
提示1 · 逻辑链
事先定容许误差→按后果区分情况→明确例外→让判断可复查。
提示2 · 猜词线索
slight wobble 和 wrong area 提示 permissible 与 unacceptable 的界线；stopping 对照 always keep moving，提示 counterexample。
显示答案 · 本段词义
tolerance：容许程度；容差；permissible：可允许的；被许可的；unacceptable：不能接受的；counterexample：反例；ambiguity：歧义；模棱两可；impartial：公正的；不偏不倚的；vague：模糊的；不明确的

Time limits also shape a test plan. Repeating one route is a tempting shortcut: it produces quantitative results without addressing a qualitative change, such as a shiny floor. The feasibility of a long test matters, but so does its purpose. Replication helps when the team has only a tentative explanation for an odd result. Checking it on a second surface may answer more than an afternoon of measurements irrelevant to the suspected cause.

🟣 表达拆解
without addressing 指数字未回答的另一类问题；so does its purpose 省略了重复的 matters；more than 比较信息价值而非花费时间。
🧠 段落回忆：时间只够做几轮时，怎样让这些测试更有信息量？
提示1 · 逻辑链
时间有限→区分重复和新变化→根据待验证的解释选择下一轮。
提示2 · 猜词线索
tempting 与 one route 提示 shortcut；only a... explanation 提示 tentative 尚未坐实。
显示答案 · 本段词义
shortcut：捷径；省事的方法；feasibility：可行性；replication：重复实验；复现；tentative：初步的；尚未确定的；irrelevant：不相关的；无关紧要的；quantitative：数量上的；定量的；qualitative：性质上的；定性的

None of these steps works when people expect a reprimand for bringing bad results to the team. A group that treats every missed delivery as evidence of a clumsy operator can become complacent about the design. The presumption that someone must be to blame makes it harder to ask why a reasonable action led to a bad outcome. A team can stay vigilant without calling every mistake reckless. It needs candor to say what went wrong while the details can still be checked.

🟣 表达拆解
treat A as evidence of B 是“把 A 当作 B 的证据”；that someone... 补充 presumption 的内容；while 表示趁记录仍可核对。
🧠 段落回忆：对失误的态度，怎样影响团队能否从测试中学习？
提示1 · 逻辑链
坏结果需要被报告→归咎个人可能掩盖设计问题→及时保留可核查的信息。
提示2 · 猜词线索
someone must be to blame 提示 presumption 是事先认定；say what went wrong 提示 candor 的坦诚。
显示答案 · 本段词义
reprimand：训斥；责备；presumption：未经证实的假定；推定；vigilant：警觉的；警惕的；reckless：鲁莽的；不顾后果的；candor：坦率；坦诚；clumsy：笨拙的；不熟练的；complacent：自满而不愿反思的

When the student team returns to the lab, a clean run is still an accomplishment. But a single run is no definitive verdict. A more defensible judgment comes from validation under the conditions in which the robot is meant to work. That judgment should be traceable to specific runs, including ones in which the robot stopped. No list of tests makes a design foolproof. The gain is knowing where confidence has been earned and where it remains illusory.

🟣 表达拆解
in which 引出实际使用条件；including ones 中的 ones 指具体运行轮次；where... and where... 对照有证据的信心和仍属错觉的信心。
🧠 段落回忆：重新看开头那次成功运行，团队现在能多知道什么？
提示1 · 逻辑链
认可单次成功→验证真实使用条件→找得到证据→分清把握和未知。
提示2 · 猜词线索
specific runs 提示 traceable 的“可追溯”；earned 和 illusory 对照，提示后者是缺乏真实依据的。
显示答案 · 本段词义
defensible：有依据的；站得住脚的；validation：验证；确认符合实际用途；traceable：可追溯的；foolproof：不易出错的；万无一失的；illusory：虚幻的；看似真实的；accomplishment：成就；完成的成果；definitive：确定的；最终的
