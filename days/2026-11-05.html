Why a River Needs Room to Spread

为什么要给河流留出空间？

主题阅读 · 744词 / 10段 / 50个新词 + 20个复习词

新词已查看 0/50　复习词已查看 0/20

先抓住核心问题，再看每段怎样推进论证。长按单词可标记或取消绿色掌握状态。

On a dry afternoon, a bench on the riverbank offers a serene spot to rest. After a downpour, the river can overflow into the grass, leaving that same bench partly under water. The floodplain is the low land where this can happen. When the water begins to recede, paths in the vicinity may still be muddy. Yet a day without access to the path tells us little about whether the land has done its job.

🟣 表达拆解
leaving... 写出河水漫进草地的结果；where this can happen 指回前一句的淹水情形；tells us little about whether... 强调单凭眼前情形无法作出判断。
🧠 段落回忆：开头为什么从同一张长椅在晴天和雨后的不同状态写起？
提示1 · 逻辑链
晴天可用→涨水后暂时被淹→重新判断这片低地的作用。
提示2 · 猜词线索
under water 提示 overflow 的结果；begins to recede 与雨后涨水相对；paths in the vicinity 指周围的路径。
显示答案 · 本段词义
riverbank：河岸；downpour：倾盆大雨；overflow：溢出；漫过边界；floodplain：河漫滩；洪泛平原；recede：退去；减弱；serene：宁静的；安详的；vicinity：附近；周边地区

The spatial pattern of rain matters, but so does the ground it falls on. An impermeable road produces more runoff than a field where rain can infiltrate the soil, if other conditions are similar. Roadside drains intercept that water and convey it to the river. The distinction is between moving water out of sight and giving it somewhere to stay. A street can dry quickly even while more water is reaching the river.

🟣 表达拆解
so does... 省略 matters；if other conditions are similar 限定比较前提；even while 并列看似矛盾却能同时发生的两个过程。
🧠 段落回忆：街道上的水排得快，为什么不等于流域里的水已经少了？
提示1 · 逻辑链
不同地面→渗入与流走的比例不同→排水把水送往河道→街道干燥不等于水消失。
提示2 · 猜词线索
road 与 soil 的对比帮助理解 impermeable 和 infiltrate；to the river 给出 convey 的去向。
显示答案 · 本段词义
impermeable：不透水的；不可渗透的；runoff：地表径流；流走的雨水；infiltrate：渗入；渗透；intercept：截住；拦截；convey：输送；传送；spatial：空间的；与空间分布有关的；distinction：区别；区分

Once water reaches the river, low terrain beside it can help mitigate a peak. If water can disperse across a broad meadow, some may enter the soil. Even when rising water begins to saturate the ground, the meadow can still provide temporary detention on the surface. The velocity varies across the flooded area, and some of the water returns to the channel later. Land that looks peripheral in dry weather can thus be part of the river during a storm.

🟣 表达拆解
Once 引出水已进入河道的阶段；Even when 说明土壤逐渐饱和也不妨碍地表暂存；thus 将机制和河边低地的作用连接起来。
🧠 段落回忆：即使土壤吸不下更多水，河边草地为什么仍可能起作用？
提示1 · 逻辑链
水漫出主河道→土壤逐渐饱和→地表仍可暂存→部分水稍后返回。
提示2 · 猜词线索
across a broad meadow 提示 disperse；temporary 和 returns... later 提示 detention 是暂留。
显示答案 · 本段词义
terrain：地形；地势；disperse：分散；散开；saturate：使浸透；使达到饱和；detention：暂时滞留；此处指滞蓄；velocity：速度；此处指流速；mitigate：减轻；缓和；peripheral：边缘的；外围的

A levee can keep water away from a residential district. But a wall that narrows the space available to the river can raise the water level on its river side. Its effect downstream depends on the shape of the valley and the timing of the flows. If water rises above the crest, it may inundate land people regarded as protected. Restraint about where to build is still part of damage prevention, even behind a wall.

🟣 表达拆解
depends on 后的两个并列因素说明影响有条件；land people regarded as protected 省略了关系代词；even behind a wall 表示已有设施也不排除其他措施。
🧠 段落回忆：堤防能保护一处居民区，为什么仍要谨慎决定在哪里建房？
提示1 · 逻辑链
局部挡水→约束河道可用空间→其他河段影响不一→水超过堤顶时保护有边界。
提示2 · 猜词线索
keep water away 提示 levee 的用途；water rises above 与 inundate 紧接，帮助推断 crest 的位置。
显示答案 · 本段词义
levee：堤防；防洪堤；downstream：向下游；在下游；crest：顶部；此处指堤顶；inundate：淹没；使泛滥；prevention：预防；防止；residential：住宅的；居住区的；restraint：克制；自我约束

To ascertain whether a project has reduced risk, engineers look beyond the protected street. They can compare discharge, the volume of water passing per second, upstream and near the confluence with a tributary. Estimation based on a single high-water mark can be misleading because it cannot show whether the two peaks arrived together. A record over time is more useful for seeing whether danger moved from one place to another.

🟣 表达拆解
the volume... 是 discharge 的同位语；because 指出单个水痕的证据缺口；for seeing whether... 说明连续记录要回答什么问题。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么光看一处水位最高时留下的痕迹，不能判断整个工程的效果？
提示1 · 逻辑链
扩大观察范围→比较不同位置的流量→保留时间信息→判断风险是否转移。
提示2 · 猜词线索
the volume of water passing per second 界定 discharge；with a tributary 提示 confluence 是两股水流相汇之处。
显示答案 · 本段词义
discharge：流量；单位时间通过的水量；upstream：在上游；向上游；confluence：汇合处；汇合；tributary：支流；estimation：估算；估计；ascertain：查明；确定；misleading：容易使人误解的

A town can keep some acreage near the river in public use without putting buildings on it. Paths and playing fields offer a civic benefit on dry days. A wetland at the lower end can support wildlife conservation as well as hold water temporarily. In a storm, water may submerge the path. That is a deliberate tradeoff: a path can be closed and repaired more easily than a street of homes. Even ornamental planting can be chosen with periodic flooding in mind.

🟣 表达拆解
keep... in public use 表示继续保留公共用途；as well as 补充同一处湿地的另一作用；with... in mind 意为“把……考虑在内”。
🧠 段落回忆：开放的河边土地怎样同时服务平日使用和涨水时的需要？
提示1 · 逻辑链
保留用地→平时可活动→暴雨允许部分受淹→把较易恢复的设施放在这里。
提示2 · 猜词线索
water may submerge the path 对应关闭和修复的情形；冒号后的两个恢复成本提示 tradeoff 的含义。
显示答案 · 本段词义
acreage：土地面积；一片土地；wetland：湿地；conservation：保护；保育；submerge：淹没；使浸入水中；tradeoff：权衡；取舍；civic：市民的；公共生活的；ornamental：装饰性的；供观赏的

Planners can demarcate the area that must stay open. A restriction on new construction can prevent encroachment there. That helps keep new homes out of danger, but it does little for the occupant of a house already near the river. A town might incentivize households to relocate voluntarily. For the offer to be meaningful, there must be permanent housing within reach of work and schools.

🟣 表达拆解
that must stay open 修饰 area；but 区分未来新建住房和现有住户；within reach of 说明新住处要满足的现实条件。
🧠 段落回忆：给河流划出空间以后，为什么还要单独考虑已经住在河边的人？
提示1 · 逻辑链
划定边界→限制新的侵占→现有住户风险仍在→搬迁需要可行的住处。
提示2 · 猜词线索
area that must stay open 提示 demarcate 的划界；voluntarily 和 offer 帮助理解 incentivize 与强制的区别。
显示答案 · 本段词义
demarcate：划定；标出界线；encroachment：逐步侵占；蚕食；incentivize：以激励措施促使；relocate：搬迁；迁移；permanent：永久的；长期的；restriction：限制；约束规定；occupant：占用者；使用某空间的人

Open land can do more than hold water. A flooded meadow may receive sediment that helps keep soil fertile, and a marsh can provide a sanctuary for wildlife. The connection between those patches matters, too. A contiguous strip of land allows water to spread and retreat as the river rises and falls. When roads or walls separate the patches, an unintended effect may be to funnel water through gaps, increasing local erosion.

🟣 表达拆解
do more than 将论证从蓄水推进到生态作用；as 表示水漫开、退去与河水涨落相伴；increasing... 补出水流被挤入缺口的可能后果。
🧠 段落回忆：几块零散的空地和一片连通的低地，为什么不一定有同样的作用？
提示1 · 逻辑链
低地能承接水与泥沙→地块连通让水自由进退→阻隔可能集中水流。
提示2 · 猜词线索
a strip of land 和 separate 互相对照，提示 contiguous；水流集中通过缺口提示 erosion 的原因。
显示答案 · 本段词义
sediment：沉积物；泥沙；fertile：肥沃的；多产的；marsh：沼泽；湿地；contiguous：相连的；毗连的；erosion：侵蚀；冲刷；sanctuary：安静的避所；庇护之处；unintended：非预期的；无意造成的

Leaving room for water does not make flood damage impossible. During an extraordinary storm, storage can fill before the river reaches its peak. Debris can clog a drainage opening, and gravel may collect where the current slows. A maintenance checklist should cover the points where water enters and leaves the open land. Keeping those openings clear cannot create extra space for an unforeseen surge. A meadow can provide relief during one flood and offer too little storage during another.

🟣 表达拆解
before 指出蓄水空间先满、河流后到峰值的先后；where the current slows 修饰砾石容易堆积的位置；one... another 对照不同洪水情形。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么一片曾经发挥过作用的低地，下一场洪水仍可能不够用？
提示1 · 逻辑链
蓄水容量有限→进出口还可能堵塞→维护能减少部分问题→不同洪水的结果仍可能不同。
提示2 · 猜词线索
debris 与 opening 的搭配提示 clog；provide relief 对比 too little storage，提示 relief 是减轻而非消除。
显示答案 · 本段词义
extraordinary：异常的；非同寻常的；clog：堵塞；阻塞；drainage：排水；排水系统；gravel：砾石；碎石；relief：缓解；减轻；checklist：核对清单；检查表；unforeseen：未预料到的

The bench illustrates how the same land can be useful both when dry and when flooded. That kind of adaptation makes the interdependence of neighborhoods easier to see. A defensible plan asks whether it can avert losses overall or merely redistribute them. The land left open is part of a collective choice about what can get wet and what must stay dry. On a fine day, its most important function may be invisible.

🟣 表达拆解
both when... and when... 对照同一片地在两种状态下的用途；whether... or... 区分总体减少损失与换地方承受损失；its 指留下的开敞土地。
🧠 段落回忆：回到河边长椅，文章用它说明了城市防洪应该怎样衡量成效？
提示1 · 逻辑链
