What a Good Pass Makes Possible

一次好传球，为什么不只是把球交出去？

主题阅读 · 725词 / 10段 / 50个新词 + 20个复习词

新词已查看 0/50　复习词已查看 0/20

先抓住核心问题，再看每段怎样推进论证。长按单词可标记或取消绿色掌握状态。

On the court, you dribble toward the basket as an opponent steps into your path. A teammate is waiting beyond the crowd, hands ready. You hesitate, then pass. From the sideline, somebody may misinterpret the choice as a lack of nerve. Yet it can be a thoughtful response to where everyone stands. The useful question is what the pass makes possible. Giving up the shot can be an active decision about where the next chance should go.

🟣 表达拆解
where everyone stands 指所有人的站位；what the pass makes possible 把评价重点放在传球创造的机会。

把球交出去，为什么不一定是逃避表现？

提示1 · 逻辑链
看到队友位置 → 判断下一次机会在哪 → 传球也可能是主动选择。
提示2 · 猜词线索
从 misinterpret 后的判断与 Yet 后的回应，区分旁观印象和实际判断。
显示答案 · 本段词义
dribble：运球；连续拍球前进；opponent：对手；反对者；court：球场；法庭；teammate：队友；hesitate：犹豫；迟疑；misinterpret：误解；错误解读；thoughtful：考虑周到的；体贴的

That decision starts with attentiveness. One useful heuristic is to glance beyond the nearest defender. A player near the perimeter may draw a guard away, acting as a decoy for somebody closer to the basket. An arm in the passing lane can obstruct what looked like an easy pass a moment earlier. You are reading a moving arrangement. The person holding the ball is only one part of it.

🟣 表达拆解
acting as… 补充说明外线球员发挥的作用；what looked like… 指刚才看来存在的机会。
🧠 段落回忆

为什么判断传球时，不能只看自己面前的防守者？

提示1 · 逻辑链
其他球员也在移动 → 防守注意力和传球线路改变 → 持球人需要看整体。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 draw… away、closer to the basket 和 an arm，推断哪些变化会打开或挡住机会。
显示答案 · 本段词义
glance：瞥一眼；迅速看；defender：防守者；辩护者；perimeter：外围；周边（篮球中指外线区域）；decoy：诱饵；吸引注意的佯动者；obstruct：阻挡；妨碍；attentiveness：留心；体贴周到；heuristic：经验法则；帮助快速判断的办法

The receiver can vacate a crowded spot and change the angle again. You might pivot to send the ball on a diagonal instead of through the crowd. A high arc may clear an arm but give a guard more time to react. You have to propel the ball toward the place the receiver is moving into. This small modification can matter; your reading of the available space must remain provisional.

🟣 表达拆解
the place the receiver is moving into 指接球人即将到达的位置；remain provisional 强调判断随局面变化而更新。
🧠 段落回忆

为什么传球不是简单瞄准队友此刻站着的地方？

提示1 · 逻辑链
接球人移动 → 角度和飞行时间改变 → 要把球送向将到达的位置。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 moving into 和 more time to react，把传球落点与所需时间连起来看。
显示答案 · 本段词义
vacate：腾出；离开（位置）；pivot：以一点为轴转动；转身；diagonal：斜线；对角线；arc：弧线；弧形；propel：推动；使向前运动；modification：改动；修改；provisional：暂定的；需要随情况修正的

Good timing can make the move look effortless. A swift pass helps a nimble receiver reach the ball before the guard recovers. But speed alone is not enough. A look toward one player can coax the guard away from another, creating a more favorable opening. The receiver should be able to catch the ball readily and keep moving. The apparent ease comes from synchrony: people acting with regard to one another’s next step.

🟣 表达拆解
with regard to 指考虑到；冒号后的 people acting… 具体说明这里的 synchrony。
🧠 段落回忆

为什么球传得快，还不一定传得好？

提示1 · 逻辑链
引导防守注意 → 创造空当 → 接球时间与接球人的移动相配合。
提示2 · 猜词线索
把 speed alone 与 catch… and keep moving 对照，判断配合除了速度还需要什么。
显示答案 · 本段词义
swift：迅速的；敏捷的；nimble：灵活敏捷的；coax：诱导；耐心引导；favorable：有利的；适宜的；synchrony：同步；协调一致；effortless：不费力的；轻松的；readily：轻易地；顺利地

The scoreboard shows how much each side has scored, but not every action that made it possible. The player who drew two guards may remain uncredited. Their unassuming movement can still be invaluable. An individualistic view of the game loses that significance if it notices only who finishes. Judgment includes seeing how somebody else’s chance was made. A basket belongs in the score even when its preparation belongs to several people.

🟣 表达拆解
who finishes 指完成最后得分动作的人；who drew two guards 回指吸引防守、却未必最后接触球的人。
🧠 段落回忆

进球以外的动作，怎样也可能改变一次进攻？

提示1 · 逻辑链
有人吸引防守 → 另一人得到机会 → 只看终结者会漏掉共同准备。
提示2 · 猜词线索
从 drew two guards 和 remain uncredited 看贡献与可见记录之间的差别。
显示答案 · 本段词义
scoreboard：记分牌；uncredited：未获认可或未被记功的；invaluable：极宝贵的；价值很高的；individualistic：个人主义的；偏重个人表现的；judgment：判断；判断力；unassuming：不张扬的；朴实的；significance：重要性；意义

That does not make passing the answer to every problem. A timid player can give the ball away simply to escape a decision. Indecision then leaves the next person with less time. If your own shot is open, taking it can be the assertive and reasonable choice. The instinct to make one more pass may be fruitless when the receiver is closely guarded. No player is exempt from deciding whether to act.

🟣 表达拆解
If your own shot is open 给出自己应当出手的条件；exempt from deciding 指不能把判断责任一并转交。
🧠 段落回忆

怎样分辨主动配合和只是怕自己出手？

提示1 · 逻辑链
先看有没有更好机会 → 额外传球可能压缩时间 → 自己也要承担该承担的动作。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 own shot is open 对照 receiver is closely guarded，判断机会究竟在哪边。
显示答案 · 本段词义
timid：胆怯的；畏缩的；indecision：犹豫不决；不能决断；assertive：自信坚定的；敢于表达和行动的；reasonable：合理的；明智的；instinct：本能；直觉；fruitless：没有成果的；徒劳的；exempt：被免除义务的；豁免的

Nor can one result settle whether a pass was wise. A fluke bounce can make a sound choice look like a blunder. A miscalculation about someone’s speed is a different issue, and needs a specific correction. An instructor can distinguish the two. Criticism that calls a player inept gives them little to change. To persevere usefully, they need to know whether to rethink the decision or improve the way they carried it out.

🟣 表达拆解
the two 回指偶然弹跳与错误估计；whether… or… 区分重新判断和改进执行。
🧠 段落回忆

传球没成功时，为什么不能只说谁不行？

提示1 · 逻辑链
先区分偶然因素与判断失误 → 找到可改的地方 → 再决定怎样继续练。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 fluke bounce、miscalculation 和 specific correction，寻找批评怎样才提供信息。
显示答案 · 本段词义
fluke：侥幸；偶然事件；blunder：严重错误；失误；miscalculation：误算；错误估计；criticism：批评；评论；inept：不熟练的；笨拙无能的；instructor：教练；指导者；persevere：坚持不懈；继续努力

To entrust someone with the next play is to expect a response, not a perfect copy of your own strongest move. Cooperative play works through mutually understood possibilities. A simple pattern can be a scaffold while two people learn which passes each can handle. Trust is conditional on paying attention to that learning. A dependable player stays available after releasing the ball. Fellowship grows through sharing the work that follows, too.

🟣 表达拆解
conditional on 指以某事为条件；the work that follows 将信任落在传球之后仍要做的事上。
🧠 段落回忆

传球之后，持球人原来的责任就结束了吗？

提示1 · 逻辑链
理解彼此能做什么 → 用简单套路练配合 → 传球后继续为后续动作做好准备。
提示2 · 猜词线索
从 strongest move、each can handle 和 stays available，推断可靠的合作如何形成。
显示答案 · 本段词义
entrust：把……托付给；委托；cooperative：合作的；协作的；mutually：相互地；彼此地；conditional：有条件的；取决于某条件的；dependable：可靠的；可依赖的；scaffold：支架；（比喻）帮助完成任务的支撑；fellowship：友谊；共同参与形成的情谊

A short drill can make that work visible. Two players rehearse the timing, then try the same choice in a scrimmage with a guard involved. A coach’s demonstration gives them a starting point, rather than an answer for every move. The recap can follow the whole play: who moved, who drew attention, who received. This analysis makes collaboration something the team can discuss and improve before returning to the next game.

🟣 表达拆解
rather than… 限定示范的作用；冒号后的三个 who 分别追踪配合链上的动作。
🧠 段落回忆

练习怎样让大家看见进球之前共同做了什么？

提示1 · 逻辑链
先练基本配合 → 加入防守变化 → 回顾完整过程并改进。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 starting point 与 an answer for every move，判断示范和实际判断的关系。
显示答案 · 本段词义
drill：练习；专项训练；rehearse：排练；反复练习；scrimmage：分组对抗练习；analysis：分析；剖析；collaboration：合作；协作；demonstration：演示；示范；recap：简要回顾；小结

At the end of the possession, the spotlight may still fall on the shooter. The assist records one contribution, while other useful actions remain less visible. Being proactive can mean giving someone else room to act. It can also require the courage to shoot when the ball comes back. Another player can reciprocate on the next trip. Belonging then means staying involved in the chance you share, with opportunities to take responsibility as well as pass it on.

🟣 表达拆解
as well as 让承担与传递责任并列；when the ball comes back 说明配合也包含再次轮到自己。
🧠 段落回忆

为什么好的团队合作既需要传出去，也需要敢接回来？

提示1 · 逻辑链
给别人机会 → 自己仍参与后续 → 再轮到自己时承担相应责任。
提示2 · 猜词线索
把 room to act 与 courage to shoot 合起来看，理解结尾怎样回应开篇的选择。
显示答案 · 本段词义
possession：控球的一段过程；占有；spotlight：聚光灯；关注的焦点；assist：助攻；帮助；proactive：主动的；积极采取行动的；courage：勇气；reciprocate：回报；以相同方式回应；belonging：归属感
