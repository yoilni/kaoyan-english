One City, Unequal Heat: Who Gets to Stay Cool?

同一座城市，不同的热风险：谁能真正获得降温保护？

Reading A · The same forecast, different risks
为什么相同天气会造成不同风险？

Imagine two people waiting for a bus on the same sweltering afternoon. One stands beneath a broad tree canopy, where branches and leaves form a roof overhead. The other waits beside a bare road, finding the heat oppressive: even standing still feels exhausting. The weather forecast is identical for both. Their experience is not. To understand the difference, we must look beyond the temperature announced for the city.

🟣 表达拆解
beneath 引出位置；where 修饰树冠下的空间，解释枝叶如何形成遮蔽。The weather forecast... / Their experience... 两个短句构成对照，为全文提出问题。
🧠 段落回忆：相同天气预报为什么可能对应不同的高温体验？
提示1 · 逻辑链
先比较两人所处的具体环境，再想一想全市一个气温数字遗漏了什么。
提示2 · 猜词线索
canopy 后的 branches and leaves form a roof 是形象解释；oppressive 后的 exhausting 说明这种热带来的感受。
显示答案 · 本段词义
sweltering：酷热难耐的；canopy：树冠；遮篷；oppressive：令人难以忍受的；压迫性的

Start with what covers the ground. Vegetation, including grass and shrubs, provides a different environment from a sealed street. Permeable ground allows rain to soak in; an impervious surface keeps it out. Porous paving contains small spaces through which water can pass. Water held in soil can later evaporate, carrying heat away. Replacing soil and plants with hard surfaces changes this process and therefore the neighborhood's heat balance.

🟣 表达拆解
Start with 把开头的问题引向第一个机制。分号两侧的 allows...in / keeps...out 构成严格对照；helping explain 是现在分词结构，说明前面这些差异的解释作用。
🧠 段落回忆：地面材料怎样通过水分条件影响社区环境？
提示1 · 逻辑链
比较雨水进入土壤与停留在硬化表面时的去向。
提示2 · 猜词线索
permeable 与 impervious 由 in / out 对照解释；porous 后的 small spaces 直接描述其结构。
显示答案 · 本段词义
permeable：可渗透的；vegetation：植被；impervious：不透水的；不受影响的；porous：多孔的；可渗透的

Buildings add another layer. A reflective roof sends more sunlight away instead of absorbing it. Insulation slows heat transfer through the roof and walls, while ventilation allows air to move through rooms. None is a universal solution: the value of moving air depends partly on conditions outside. The point is that a building's design changes the heat its occupants experience, rather than merely recording the weather around it.

🟣 表达拆解
None 指前面列出的几种措施。rather than 后接 merely recording，与前面的 changes 对比，强调建筑会改变人的体验。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么建筑设计会改变居住者实际承受的热量？
提示1 · 逻辑链
找出屋顶、墙壁、空气流动分别对应的作用，再留意作者加上的限制。
提示2 · 猜词线索
reflective 对应 sends sunlight away；insulation 对应 slows heat transfer；ventilation 对应 air to move。
显示答案 · 本段词义
ventilation：通风；insulation：隔热；绝缘；reflective：反射的；深思的

The effects do not end at sunset. Walls and roads can radiate heat they stored during the day, releasing it after direct sunlight has disappeared. This nocturnal warmth matters because a bedroom may remain stifling, with hot, still air, long into the night. A person who escaped the midday sun may therefore face a second period of exposure at home. Daytime temperature alone misses that part of the story.

🟣 表达拆解
they stored during the day 是省略关系代词的定语从句，修饰 heat。may therefore 表示基于前文的推论，而不是声称每个人必然如此。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么只看白天气温会低估居民承受的高温？
提示1 · 逻辑链
把“白天储热”与“夜间释放”连起来，再考虑人回家后的环境。
提示2 · 猜词线索
radiate 后的 releasing it 给出近义解释；nocturnal 与 night 呼应；stifling 后的 hot, still air 提供具体感受。
显示答案 · 本段词义
nocturnal：夜间的；radiate：辐射；散发；stifling：闷热难耐的

The body needs opportunities to recover. Perspiration, or sweating, helps remove heat when moisture evaporates from the skin, but humid air makes that process harder. Rest in a cooler place can be restorative, giving the body time to recuperate before another demanding day. People can acclimatize to heat, becoming more accustomed to it over time. That adjustment, however, does not make repeated exposure harmless or remove the need for protection.

🟣 表达拆解
or sweating 是同位解释。That adjustment 回指上一句的逐渐适应；however 引出限制，避免从“能适应”错误推到“不需要保护”。
🧠 段落回忆：作者为什么在介绍人体适应能力后，马上补充一句限制？
提示1 · 逻辑链
区分“适应能力有所提高”和“风险完全消失”。
提示2 · 猜词线索
perspiration 用 sweating 解释；restorative 与 recuperate 都围绕 recover；acclimatize 后的 accustomed 提供线索。
显示答案 · 本段词义
acclimatize：逐渐适应新环境；restorative：恢复性的；perspiration：汗；出汗；recuperate：恢复健康或体力

Nor does everyone have equal freedom to take a break. Someone doing strenuous work, such as carrying heavy materials, generates extra body heat while remaining in the sun. Hydration means replacing lost fluids; respite means a temporary break from that burden. Both are preventive measures, intended to reduce danger before an emergency develops. Advice is of limited use if a worker cannot act on it without losing pay.

🟣 表达拆解
Nor 放在句首引起倒装：does everyone...。without losing pay 是限制条件；最后一句把生理问题推进到工作制度问题。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么知道应该休息，并不等于劳动者就能得到保护？
提示1 · 逻辑链
从“需要做什么”转向“有没有条件去做”。
提示2 · 猜词线索
strenuous 由 carrying heavy materials 举例；hydration 和 respite 都有 means 解释；preventive 由 before an emergency 提示。
显示答案 · 本段词义
strenuous：费力的；繁重的；hydration：补水；保持体内水分；respite：短暂休息；暂时缓解；preventive：预防性的

Housing and income deepen this gap. Susceptibility to harm varies with health, but deprivation also matters: a household lacking money may have fewer ways to escape a hot room. The burden can fall disproportionately on disadvantaged residents, who carry more of the risk while having fewer resources to respond. Their choices are restricted before the warning arrives. Describing the outcome simply as a failure to follow advice overlooks those restrictions.

🟣 表达拆解
while 把 more risk 与 fewer resources 对照起来。Describing... 作主语；those restrictions 回指前文受到资源限制的选择。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么不能把高温伤害简单归因于居民不听建议？
提示1 · 逻辑链
先找出限制选择的条件，再判断这些条件是否由一次提醒就能改变。
提示2 · 猜词线索
deprivation 后的 lacking money 提供例子；disproportionately 由 more risk / fewer resources 的不对称关系解释。
显示答案 · 本段词义
susceptibility：易感性；敏感性；deprivation：匮乏；剥夺；disadvantaged：处境不利的；disproportionately：不成比例地；格外地

This is why heat planning is also a question of inequity: protection is not shared fairly. A cooler city is an attainable goal, but lowering an average temperature is not enough if the most exposed residents remain unprotected. Preparedness requires an anticipatory approach: identify those residents and arrange help before the next hot spell. Reading the forecast is only the beginning. The harder question is whether the city can turn that knowledge into protection.

🟣 表达拆解
This is why 承接前面环境、劳动和住房的差异。if 从句限定 not enough 的条件；最后一句把 Reading A 的解释转化为 Reading B 要回答的问题。
🧠 段落回忆：城市降温政策应该追求什么，不能只追求什么？
提示1 · 逻辑链
比较“全市平均值改善”与“风险最高的人获得保护”。
提示2 · 猜词线索
inequity 后直接解释为 not shared fairly；attainable 与 goal 搭配；anticipatory 后的 before 提示行动时间。
显示答案 · 本段词义
inequity：不公平；preparedness：准备状态；attainable：可以达到的；anticipatory：预先的；预期性的

Reading B · From cooler buildings to fairer protection
为什么有技术，保护仍然难以落实？

Consider a rented dwelling: a home whose occupants do not own it. Making it habitable during hot weather may require roof repairs, better shading or the installation of cooling equipment. The technical rationale is straightforward: reduce the heat entering the room or improve its removal. Yet identifying a useful measure does not tell us who will pay for it. That unanswered question can leave an apparently practical improvement waiting for years.

🟣 表达拆解
a home... 是 dwelling 的同位解释。Yet 把技术可行性与费用承担对立起来；who will pay 是 tell us 的宾语从句。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么技术上可行的住房改善方案仍可能迟迟不能实施？
提示1 · 逻辑链
将“知道怎么改”与“确定谁付钱”分开考虑。
提示2 · 猜词线索
dwelling 后用 home 解释；habitable 与 making a home... 对应；installation 结合 cooling equipment 理解为把设备装好。
显示答案 · 本段词义
dwelling：住宅；居所；habitable：适宜居住的；installation：安装；rationale：基本原理；理由

A tenant may pay the electricity bill, while the landlord must approve and finance major building work. Savings then accrue to the resident: the benefits build up in that person's bills rather than the owner's account. This creates a disincentive for the owner to invest. A short tenancy can weaken the resident's willingness to contribute as well, because the household may move before recovering its share of the cost.

🟣 表达拆解
while 对比承担电费和承担改造费用的两方。as well 表示不只有房东缺动力，租户也可能不愿出资；because 给出第二种动力不足的原因。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么房东和租户可能都不愿主动出资改造？
提示1 · 逻辑链
分别追踪房东与租户的支出、收益和可等待的时间。
提示2 · 猜词线索
tenant 由与 landlord 的分工推断；accrue 后的 build up 是解释；disincentive 从“出钱者不直接获益”推断；tenancy 由 short 和 move 提示。
显示答案 · 本段词义
tenant：租户；tenancy：租期；租用关系；disincentive：抑制因素；使人不愿行动的因素；accrue：逐渐增加；累积归于

A policy response should address that mismatch rather than assume either side is simply careless. Reimbursement of part of a verified expense could reduce an owner's reluctance to invest. Support paid in advance may be more useful for someone unable to incur the initial cost at all. But an affordable program must work for residents as well as owners; public assistance achieves little if the resulting rent increase leaves the household worse off.

🟣 表达拆解
rather than 指出应避免的解释。could / may 表示可考虑的政策路径，不是在宣称已有统一制度。as well as 强调方案必须同时考虑两方。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么补贴设计既要考虑支付时间，也要考虑租金后果？
提示1 · 逻辑链
先问参与者是否垫得起钱，再看最终费用是否转嫁给住户。
提示2 · 猜词线索
reimbursement 与 verified expense 搭配，指费用发生后的返还；reluctance 接 to invest；incur 与 initial cost 搭配。
显示答案 · 本段词义
affordable：负担得起的；reimbursement：报销；偿付；reluctance：不情愿；勉强；incur：招致；承担

That danger makes continued occupancy important: can the original household remain in the improved home? A policy could link public support to clear tenancy protections and monitor whether renovation is followed by eviction or forced relocation. Such conditions would not make every dispute disappear. They would, however, confront a basic contradiction: a project intended to protect vulnerable residents should not achieve a better building by pushing those same residents out.

🟣 表达拆解
a project intended to... 中 intended to... 修饰 project。by pushing... 指实现结果的手段；those same residents 强调受益对象与被排除对象竟是同一群人。
🧠 段落回忆：评估改造成功时，为什么不能只检查建筑变好了没有？
提示1 · 逻辑链
除了改造后的建筑，也追踪改造前住在里面的人。
提示2 · 猜词线索
occupancy 后有 remain in the home 的解释；eviction / relocation 与结尾 pushing residents out 联系起来猜。
显示答案 · 本段词义
tenancy：租期；租用关系；relocation：搬迁；迁移；eviction：驱逐；逐出租屋；occupancy：居住；占用

The same attention to costs is needed in public spaces. Officials may procure shade structures or cooling equipment, but purchase is only the first payment. Upkeep brings recurrent costs: cleaning, repairs and staff time return year after year. With a finite budget, a city must compare what it can keep working, not merely what it can afford to open. A modest service that survives each summer may protect more people than an impressive but short-lived launch.

🟣 表达拆解
not merely 把比较标准从开办费用扩展到持续运转能力。what it can keep working 是 compare 的宾语从句。最后一句用 may 作有条件判断。
🧠 段落回忆：为什么开办成本较低，不一定意味着一个项目长期负担得起？
提示1 · 逻辑链
把一次购买与逐年重复的支出分开，再考虑预算限制。
提示2 · 猜词线索
procure 后接设备，结合 purchase 推断；upkeep 后列出 cleaning / repairs；recurrent 由 year after year 解释；finite 对应预算约束。
显示答案 · 本段词义
procure：采购；获得；upkeep：维护；保养；recurrent：反复发生的；finite：有限的

Trees illustrate the problem. Planting them can be easier to fund than caring for them. Young trees may wither without adequate water, and a dry site may need irrigation to replenish moisture that has been lost. If deterioration continues, costly restoration may replace routine care. The lesson is not to reject planting. It is to treat the people, water and money needed afterward as part of the original project, rather than as an optional extra.

🟣 表达拆解
Planting / caring 两个动名词比较建设与养护。not to... / It is to... 先排除误读，再给出主张；afterward 回指种植完成之后。
🧠 段落回忆：种树的例子怎样说明维护应当在立项时就被考虑？
提示1 · 逻辑链
连接“后续资源不足”“状态变差”“后期修复代价”三个环节。
提示2 · 猜词线索
wither 与 without water 联系；replenish 后有 moisture lost；deterioration 与 costly restoration 构成问题和补救关系。
显示答案 · 本段词义
deterioration：恶化；replenish：补充；重新装满；wither：枯萎；衰弱；restoration：恢复；修复

Even a well-maintained service can miss its intended users. A city might repurpose a library as a cooling center, yet residents may not know its opening hours or how to reach it. Outreach means actively contacting people rather than waiting for them to arrive. If seats are limited, officials may have to ration access, deciding how to share scarce places. Those decisions should reflect need, not simply reward whoever hears about the service first.

🟣 表达拆解
rather than 对比主动接触和被动等待。whoever hears... 意为无论谁先得知信息；not simply reward 指出先到先得不一定符合实际需要。
🧠 段落回忆：服务建好了，为什么仍可能帮助不到最需要的人？
提示1 · 逻辑链
分别考虑信息、到达条件和有限容量这三道障碍。
提示2 · 猜词线索
repurpose 由 library as a cooling center 解释用途变化；outreach 后有 means；ration 后的 share scarce places 解释分配含义。
显示答案 · 本段词义
outreach：主动联系并提供服务；ration：定量供应；限量分配；repurpose：改变用途；重新利用

Finally, a resilient system needs a plan for failure. An outage should trigger a clear alternative, not leave residents outside a locked cooling center. An adaptive city also asks who actually received help, who was missed and what should change next summer. Return to the two people at the bus stop: success is not just a cooler average on a map. It is a smaller gap between the protection available to each of them.

🟣 表达拆解
not leave 与 trigger 并列，共同受 should 支配。who... / who... / what... 三个从句作 asks 的宾语。Return to 首尾照应，把抽象评价落实到人的处境。
🧠 段落回忆：本文最终用什么标准判断城市降温是否成功？

resilient 对应 failure 后仍有 alternative；outage 与 locked cooling center 暗示服务中断；adaptive 对应根据遗漏者调整下一年的行动。
显示答案 · 本段词义
