The First Line You Write on Your Own

为什么听懂了课，独立做题却卡住？

主题阅读 · 733词 / 10段 / 50个新词 + 20个复习词

新词已查看 0/50　复习词已查看 0/20

先抓住核心问题，再看每段怎样推进论证。长按单词可标记或取消绿色掌握状态。

Following a calculus lecture can feel effortless at ten at night. You can follow the lecturer’s moves readily, and the passage from one line to the next seems seamless. Then the video ends, you open the problem set, and the empty space under the first question brings a surprising degree of apprehension. The earlier ease can be deceptive. When somebody else keeps the calculation moving, it is easy to misjudge the source of that fluency.

🟣 表达拆解
keep the calculation moving 是「keep + 宾语 + 现在分词」结构，写的是谁在持续推进计算。
🧠 段落回忆

刚才明明都听懂了，为什么打开习题却突然紧张？

提示1 · 逻辑链
跟随算式 → 视频结束 → 必须自己起步。
提示2 · 猜词线索
从 one line to the next 看衔接方式；用同一人在两种场景的感受反差猜最后三句。
显示答案 · 本段词义
effortless：不费力的；轻松的；readily：轻易地；顺利地；deceptive：有欺骗性的；容易使人误判的；misjudge：误判；错误估计；fluency：流畅；熟练；seamless：无缝的；衔接自然的；apprehension：忧虑；不安

A good demonstration makes the decisions behind each line easy to miss. The instructor has already chosen which terms to group and which notation to keep. A sequential display can emphasize the lines that matter and leave out the false starts. You can annotate every line and learn from it. The example still serves as a scaffold; you have yet to find out which parts of the work it is doing for you.

🟣 表达拆解
have yet to find out 是「还没有弄清」；which parts 引导 find out 的宾语从句。
🧠 段落回忆

讲得清楚的例题可能替学习者做了什么？

提示1 · 逻辑链
老师先做选择 → 板书呈现结果 → 学生沿成形的路线走。
提示2 · 猜词线索
从 group、keep 和 leave out 读出老师的取舍，再想例题替学习者撑住了哪一部分工作。
显示答案 · 本段词义
demonstration：演示；示范；instructor：教师；指导者；notation：记号；符号体系；annotate：给……加注释或批注；scaffold：支架；（比喻）帮助完成任务的支撑；sequential：按顺序排列的；依次的；emphasize：强调；突出

Once a substitution appears on the board, the derivation is easy to follow. Working alone, you must formulate a plan before you know which procedure will work. One heuristic is to look for an expression alongside its derivative. You might have to rearrange the terms to see the pair. It is a provisional choice: a match in one part of the integral does not imply that the rest will fit.

🟣 表达拆解
Working alone 的逻辑主语是 you；before 从句点明动手制定方案时，还不知道哪套步骤可行。
🧠 段落回忆

为什么会看推导，仍然不等于会选择换元？

提示1 · 逻辑链
先有换元 → 能跟着算；先遇到题目 → 要自己判断能否换元。
提示2 · 猜词线索
look for 写出找线索的办法；冒号后的 does not 暗示这条线索不能保证成功。
显示答案 · 本段词义
derivation：推导；推导过程；formulate：拟定；形成（计划、想法等）；procedure：程序；做事的步骤；heuristic：帮助解题的经验法则；provisional：暂定的；临时的；rearrange：重新排列；重新安排；imply：暗示；隐含

Psychologists Henry Roediger and Jeffrey Karpicke asked students to read passages and then either reread them or try to reconstruct them from memory. More rereading helped on a test five minutes later. After two days or a week, retrieval led to better recall. The rereaders had been more confident about remembering. The finding puts that certainty in perspective, though a test of memory for textual material tells us little about becoming proficient at calculus.

🟣 表达拆解
had been 把自信放回延迟测试之前；though 从句限定这个实验结果可以解释到什么程度。
🧠 段落回忆

当时觉得记得牢，与几天后实际记得牢，为什么不是一回事？

提示1 · 逻辑链
重读与回想 → 五分钟与几天后 → 主观预期与实际表现。
提示2 · 猜词线索
从 from memory 猜学习时做了什么；用 five minutes 与 two days or a week 对照结果。
显示答案 · 本段词义
retrieval：提取；检索（此处指从记忆中提取）；recall：回忆；记起的能力；confident：有把握的；自信的；textual：文本的；与文字内容有关的；proficient：熟练的；精通的；reconstruct：重构；凭记忆复原；perspective：恰当的看法；put … in perspective：把……放回更大的背景中看

An unaided attempt may elicit only a fragment of a solution, but that can be enough to pinpoint an impasse. Say you can choose a substitution but leave the next line blank because you cannot express the remaining terms in the new variable. Even this incomplete attempt tells you which step to troubleshoot when you reopen the example. It is a smaller problem than the one you started with.

🟣 表达拆解
Say you can... 的 say 引入假设场景，意思接近 suppose；which step to troubleshoot 是「疑问词 + 不定式」。
🧠 段落回忆

只做出第一步的尝试，能给重看例题的人什么帮助？

提示1 · 逻辑链
试着开头 → 发现确切断点 → 带着具体问题回看。
提示2 · 猜词线索
leave the next line 之后的原因，提示断点在哪里；最后一句指出回看范围怎样变了。
显示答案 · 本段词义
pinpoint：准确找出；精确定位；impasse：僵局；无法继续的困境；blank：空白的；未填的；incomplete：不完整的；未完成的；troubleshoot：查找并解决问题；排查故障；elicit：引出；使产生（反应、回答等）；fragment：片段；碎片

Once you find the step, the temptation is to transcribe the solution. You might remember the lecturer’s words verbatim and still only mimic the calculation. To paraphrase the reasoning, you need to say why the remaining terms must be written in the new variable. That can condense a page of symbols into a single reason. Leave the extraneous markings aside. The coherence of your account depends on how you connect the lines.

🟣 表达拆解
still only 把「背得准确」和「依旧只是模仿」并列在同一人身上；depends on 后接 how 引导的从句。
🧠 段落回忆

重看卡住的那一步时，抄对与讲清有什么区别？

提示1 · 逻辑链
照抄答案 → 可能复现外形；找出变换理由 → 能解释两行关系。
提示2 · 猜词线索
把 words verbatim 与 say why 对照，再用 a page 和 a single reason 猜压缩的动作。
显示答案 · 本段词义
transcribe：抄录；转写；verbatim：逐字地；一字不差地；mimic：模仿；仿效；paraphrase：用自己的话转述；condense：压缩；浓缩；extraneous：无关的；多余的；coherence：连贯；条理性

A small modification to the example makes for a more persuasive test than repeating it. An analogous problem may use different symbols but preserve the structure. Another variant may foreground the same expression while leaving out the factor that made substitution useful. In the first case you have to recognize a route through an unfamiliar problem. In the second, you have to know when to let go of one. Both show how transferable your understanding is.

🟣 表达拆解
make for 在这里是「使得……成为、带来」；while leaving out 写的是同一变式同时保留表面线索、撤掉关键条件。
🧠 段落回忆

为什么一道看起来很像的变式，反而可能需要换一种做法？

提示1 · 逻辑链
表面不同、结构相同 → 可以迁移；表面相似、条件改变 → 需要放弃旧做法。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 different symbols / preserve the structure 与 the same expression / leaving out 对比两种变式。
显示答案 · 本段词义
modification：改动；修改；analogous：相似的；可类比的；variant：变体；变式；unfamiliar：不熟悉的；陌生的；transferable：可迁移的；可转用的；persuasive：有说服力的；foreground：突出；使处于显眼位置

Difficulty is an equivocal sign. It is worth learning to tolerate the frustration of a problem while there is still a step you can try. If the obstacle is a rule you never learned, a few more minutes might be fruitless. A hint can increase the likelihood of finding a way forward while leaving the rest of the solution in your hands. It gives you a reason to persevere, since there is now something different to try.

🟣 表达拆解
leave something in your hands 是「把某事留给你处理」；while leaving 写的是提示能帮你起步，同时把后续工作留给你。
🧠 段落回忆

什么时候值得再试一步，什么时候需要一条提示？

提示1 · 逻辑链
还有可试的路 → 继续；缺少必要规则 → 要一个够用的提示。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 a step you can try 和 a rule you never learned 判断两种困难为何不同。
显示答案 · 本段词义
tolerate：忍受；容许；frustration：受挫感；沮丧；fruitless：没有成果的；徒劳的；obstacle：障碍；阻碍；persevere：坚持不懈；继续努力；equivocal：含义不明的；可有不同解释的；likelihood：可能性；概率

That kind of attempt need not take over a study session. Pause after one example and allot two minutes to start the next problem. Write what you can justify and mark the first step you cannot. The interruption in playback gives you a question to carry into the rest of the lecture. The next day, start your recap by seeing how far your recollection takes you before you look back.

🟣 表达拆解
the first step you cannot 省去了前面已出现的 justify；how far your recollection takes you 问的是凭记忆能推进多远。

两分钟的暂停，怎样改变之后听课和复习时看的东西？

提示1 · 逻辑链
先动手 → 找到一个具体问题 → 带着它继续听并隔日再试。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 two minutes 与 take over 对照所需时间；从 before you look back 判断回顾的顺序。
显示答案 · 本段词义
session：一段活动时间；一次学习或训练；allot：划拨；分配（时间、资源等）；interruption：中断；打断；playback：（音视频的）播放；回放；recap：简要回顾；小结；pause：暂停；暂时停下；recollection：回忆；记忆

A viewing log can portray a productive evening right down to the minute. It gives the reassurance of having something measurable to show for your time, but it says little about your reliance on the person at the board. Under that first question is a more meaningful record: a line you can now write without help. It is a small increment of independence. The composure you feel when you write it comes from knowing you can begin.

🟣 表达拆解
right down to the minute 强调记录精确到分钟；Under that first question is... 用地点开头，把目光带回开篇习题的那片空白。

为什么自己写出的一行，比看完一小时的记录更有分量？
