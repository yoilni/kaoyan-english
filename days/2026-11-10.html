When Is a Pair of Headphones Really Worn Out?

一副耳机什么时候才算用完？

主题阅读 · 707词 / 10段 / 50个新词 + 20个复习词

新词已查看 0/50　复习词已查看 0/20

先抓住核心问题，再看每段怎样推进论证。长按单词可标记或取消绿色掌握状态。

The hinge on your headphones cracks when you pull them from your bag. One cup drops abruptly. The music still plays, but the pair is unusable on the walk to class. In the first moment of frustration, it is almost a reflex to search for a replacement. The scale of the damage is deceptive: everything seems to have stopped working at once, though most of the device’s functionality is still there.

🟣 表达拆解
though 让步从句提醒读者区分「整机看似坏掉」和「仍有功能」；at once 在这里是「一下子」。

耳机戴不住了，为什么仍值得先看看哪里坏了？

提示1 · 逻辑链
转轴出问题 → 整副耳机暂时难用 → 其余功能仍在。
提示2 · 猜词线索
从 still plays 和 unusable 的对照判断故障看起来有多大，再读 at once 后的让步。
显示答案 · 本段词义
hinge：铰链；转轴；abruptly：突然地；猝然地；unusable：无法使用的；reflex：反射动作；本能反应；functionality：功能；实用功能；frustration：受挫感；沮丧；deceptive：有欺骗性的；容易使人误判的

The constituent parts of a pair age unevenly. The joint bears stress each time you spread the band, a repetitive load the speakers never have to carry. A crack may grow by a small increment each time. The result can be asymmetrical: one joint has a flaw while the other still holds. What happens to the rest depends on whether the joint can be treated as a part in its own right.

🟣 表达拆解
a repetitive load 是对前面 stress 的补充；in its own right 强调把转动处本身视为一个独立部件。
🧠 段落回忆

同一副耳机里，为什么转动处和扬声器不一定同时坏掉？

提示1 · 逻辑链
各部件承担的工作不同 → 磨损速度不同 → 局部坏不等于整体报废。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 each time 和 never have to carry 推断哪部分反复受力，再看 one joint 与 the other 的对照。
显示答案 · 本段词义
constituent：构成整体的；组成的；unevenly：不均匀地；不一致地；stress：应力；压力；repetitive：反复的；重复性的；asymmetrical：不对称的；increment：增量；一点增加；flaw：缺陷；瑕疵

Before deciding whether to disassemble anything, you have to ascertain what the crack has changed. A superficial scratch may look ugly without affecting fit. A cup that swings beyond its usual limit is an unmistakable sign that the joint has given way. You can locate the break without trying to dislodge the parts around it. To troubleshoot a break, it helps to know how much is still working.

🟣 表达拆解
has given way 在此指连接处撑不住、发生损坏；without trying to… 表示定位故障不以拆动其他部件为前提。

为什么排查时也要看哪些部件还正常？

提示1 · 逻辑链
区分表面磨损和实际失效 → 找到故障范围 → 决定是否动其他部分。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 without affecting fit 对照 swings beyond its usual limit，判断哪种变化只在表面。
显示答案 · 本段词义
disassemble：拆开；拆卸；superficial：表面的；肤浅的；unmistakable：一看便知的；不会认错的；locate：确定……的位置；找出；dislodge：使脱离原位；撬动；ascertain：查明；确定；troubleshoot：查找并解决问题；排查故障

A designer can omit a visible fastener by choosing to weld two pieces of plastic together. The casing looks clean; there is no screw to lose. The same joint may make a damaged part inaccessible when the headphones need attention. Opening it can require an irreversible break. The obstacle then has little to do with the size of the original crack. It was built into the way the pieces meet.

🟣 表达拆解
have little to do with 是「与……关系不大」；was built into 指障碍源自设计时决定的连接方式。

为什么一个小故障也可能很难修？

提示1 · 逻辑链
省去外露紧固件 → 外壳整洁 → 维修时难以无损打开。
提示2 · 猜词线索
把 no screw to lose 与 opening it can require… 对照，理解同一设计在两种情境下的代价。
显示答案 · 本段词义
fastener：紧固件；扣件；weld：焊接；熔接；casing：外壳；罩壳；inaccessible：难以到达或接触的；irreversible：不可逆的；无法恢复的；omit：省略；遗漏；obstacle：障碍；阻碍

The Fairbuds XL manual, for example, labels the replaceable ear cushions and the removable headband in a diagram. The maker’s repair documentation includes a demonstration of exchanging individual parts. The damaged piece can be treated as a module, with an outline you can see before the work starts. The procedure ends when its replacement is in place; the owner does not need to disturb the rest of the device.

🟣 表达拆解
treat A as B 表示「把 A 当作 B 处理」；in place 表示配件已经装到位。

设计成可独立更换，改变了维修的哪一步？

提示1 · 逻辑链
图示标明可拆件 → 单独处理损坏件 → 其他部件留在原位。
提示2 · 猜词线索
从 individual parts 和 the rest of the device 判断更换的范围。
显示答案 · 本段词义
replaceable：可更换的；可替代的；removable：可拆卸的；可移除的；diagram：示意图；图解；documentation：说明资料；文档；module：可独立处理或更换的组件；模块；demonstration：演示；示范；procedure：程序；操作步骤

The feasibility of such a modification depends on the rest of the design. A larger joint might be easier to open but make the headphones bulky. The convenience of a compact, lightweight pair matters to someone who carries it all day. To engineer a device for repair is to ask how to make the joint reachable while keeping the features that made the pair useful in the first place.

🟣 表达拆解
To engineer… is to ask… 用两个不定式说明设计的实际任务；in the first place 回指耳机本来好用的原因。

为什么不是把接合处做大、做得容易打开就够了？

提示1 · 逻辑链
改善维修入口 → 可能增加体积 → 还要兼顾日常携带。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 carries it all day 推断 compact 和 lightweight 的价值，再看 while keeping 保留了什么。
显示答案 · 本段词义
bulky：体积大的；笨重的；convenience：便利；方便；compact：小巧紧凑的；lightweight：轻便的；重量轻的；engineer：设计并制造；精心设计；feasibility：可行性；modification：改动；修改

Even a joint you can reach is of little use if the right spare is not obtainable. Two headbands can look alike even if one is meant for a different variant of the headphones. A shared identifier on the old part and its package gives the owner an unambiguous way to check the match. When a manufacturer decides to discontinue a spare, the owner’s reliance on a single supplier becomes clear.

🟣 表达拆解
of little use 指作用有限；be meant for 指为某个型号设计或适配。

为什么能打开耳机，仍不足以保证能修？

提示1 · 逻辑链
能接触坏件 → 还需匹配的配件 → 核对型号并确认有货。
提示2 · 猜词线索
用 look alike 和 different variant 的反差理解编号的用途；从 single supplier 想停供会怎样。
显示答案 · 本段词义
obtainable：可取得的；可买到的；identifier：用来识别的编号或标记；unambiguous：明确的；不含歧义的；manufacturer：制造商；生产厂商；discontinue：停止生产、供应或使用；variant：变体；不同型号；reliance：依赖；依靠

Repair does not always make sense. Damage in several places can make the work impractical. A pair that has become unsafe to use may warrant a look from a qualified technician. Some breaks are irreparable, and replacement is then defensible. The point of having a choice is to see when repair is worthwhile. One damaged joint on a pair that still sounds right is a different matter.

🟣 表达拆解
may warrant a look 表示情况可能值得进一步查看；a different matter 把末句拉回单一部件坏掉的情形。

什么情况下换掉耳机是合理选择，什么情况值得考虑维修？

提示1 · 逻辑链
多处失效或存在安全问题 → 可能不宜修；大部分仍好 → 单独判断坏件。
提示2 · 猜词线索
从 damage in several places 对照 one damaged joint，分辨作者讨论的两种情形。
显示答案 · 本段词义
impractical：不切实际的；难以实行的；unsafe：不安全的；qualified：有资质的；合格的；irreparable：无法修复的；无法弥补的；worthwhile：值得做的；有价值的；warrant：使……成为必要；值得采取某种措施；defensible：有依据的；站得住脚的

No one has to master a new trade for this option to be approachable. A short checklist can tell you which part fits, what belongs in the toolkit, and where the job might exceed your competence. A picture of the part before removal can be instructive. The reassurance comes from seeing the size of the job before setting aside an evening. That can be enough to turn a vague willingness into a decision.

🟣 表达拆解
which、what、where 引导的三个宾语从句并列，分别回答配件、工具和能力范围；set aside 表示留出时间。

When the headphones fit again, the old scratches are still there. This is a small act of renewal, without the thrill of taking a new pair out of a box. Extending their lifetime is a measurable gain. The more meaningful change may be that they no longer seem expendable at the first sign of wear. You can appreciate a familiar object without pretending it will last forever. Attachment gives you a reason to notice what still works.

🟣 表达拆解
