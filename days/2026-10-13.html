Why Bread Dough Rises

面团为什么会膨胀？

单一主题 · 687词 / 10段 / 50个新词 + 20个复习词

新词已查看 0/50　复习词已查看 0/20

沿着酵母产气、面筋留气和温度影响读下去。先猜词、回忆主旨，再展开提示与翻译；长按切换绿色掌握状态。

You leave a batch of bread dough in a bowl. An hour later, it has begun to inflate, growing larger without any new flour. Fermentation is responsible for this familiar culinary change. Yet the time required is variable, and the chronology matters: mixing comes before rising, and baking follows. If the dough fails to rise, the bread may remain dense. What makes the same ingredients develop such a different structure?

🟣 表达拆解
without any new flour 强调体积变化并非加入材料造成；such a different structure 引出后文要解释的核心问题。

提示1 · 逻辑链

提示2 · 猜词线索

The starting point is yeast, a living organism made of single cells. Its microscopic size hides its activity from ordinary sight. Dry yeast can remain dormant until suitable conditions activate it. Water and a nutrient supply support its metabolic reactions. A cellular membrane regulates exchanges with the surroundings. The rising dough therefore reflects the work of tiny living cells, even though those cells themselves are invisible.

🟣 表达拆解
made of single cells 修饰 organism；until 标记激活发生的条件边界；even though 引出观察不到细胞的让步。

提示1 · 逻辑链
区分细胞本身的可见性与活动造成的体积变化。
提示2 · 猜词线索

显示答案 · 本段词义
yeast：酵母；organism：生物；有机体；microscopic：微小的；显微镜下的；cellular：细胞的；activate：激活；使活动；membrane：膜；薄膜；nutrient：营养物；营养元素；metabolic：代谢的；dormant：休眠的；暂时不活跃的

Flour contains starch, a carbohydrate built from linked sugar units. An enzyme can break some of it into smaller sugars. Yeast then uses sugars such as glucose to obtain energy. These soluble materials dissolve in water and nourish the cells. Yeast can digest sugars through anaerobic fermentation, without oxygen. Carbon dioxide is one byproduct of this process. The crucial result for bread is a growing supply of gas.

🟣 表达拆解

🧠 段落回忆：面粉中的物质，怎样最终变成使面团膨胀的气体？
提示1 · 逻辑链

提示2 · 猜词线索

Gas production alone cannot explain the rise. Flour protein forms gluten when mixed with water and worked. This creates a cohesive network whose parts hold together. It can entrap gas rather than let every bubble escape. The accumulation of bubbles expands the dough. The network must also be extensible, able to stretch without immediately tearing. Bread rises because gas is produced and a suitable structure holds it.

🟣 表达拆解

🧠 段落回忆：为什么产气和留住气体必须同时发生？
提示1 · 逻辑链

提示2 · 猜词线索

显示答案 · 本段词义

This explains why bakers knead the dough. Repeated folding and pressing blend the ingredients and develop the network. Moisture helps flour particles adhere as they mix. Gradually, the dough can become supple, bending and stretching more easily. It needs enough tension to hold its shape. Excessive rigidity, however, would resist expansion. The useful balance is between a structure that stretches and one that keeps the gas inside.

🟣 表达拆解

Temperature changes the speed of this process. Lukewarm conditions can support more vigorous activity than a cold room. A chill slows fermentation, while excessive heat can scald and kill yeast. Cells are susceptible to temperature damage. Differences in rising speed can stem from these conditions. A thermometer measures what touch only estimates. A temperature gradient between the surface and center can also make one part warmer than another.

🟣 表达拆解

The surface needs attention too. Water can evaporate into dry air, causing dehydration and a stiff skin. A protective cover helps conserve moisture while the dough rests. Surface preservation supports its ability to stretch later. The container need not be completely airtight to reduce water loss. The purpose is to prevent drying, rather than imprison every gas molecule. A surface that remains flexible can expand with the dough underneath.

🟣 表达拆解

Waiting longer does not guarantee a better rise. A sturdy network must still support the expanding bubbles. With excessive fermentation, degradation can weaken that structure. Gas may diffuse outward, spreading from crowded bubbles toward the surrounding air. If the supporting walls fail, the gas can dissipate and the dough may deflate. The visible rise therefore has a limit. More gas is helpful only while the structure can retain it.

🟣 表达拆解

When you retrieve the bowl and uncover the dough, a changed smell may accompany its rise. Fermentation produces volatile compounds that contribute to aroma. Baking can make the bread fragrant while the gas inside initially expands further. Heat then sets the internal structure. At the surface, a crust forms as water is lost. It may become crisp, contrasting with the softer interior. The enlarged dough has become a lasting structure.

🟣 表达拆解

Return to the bowl that seemed to grow by itself. The change was progressive, developing through several connected stages. In cold conditions, this stage may linger, while baking prematurely interrupts the rise. It is easy to underestimate the importance of structure when only size is visible. A balloon offers a useful analogy: gas needs something that can stretch and contain it. Understanding both requirements explains success and failure through the same simple mechanism.

🟣 表达拆解
