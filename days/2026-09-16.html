The New Industrial Map Is Drawn by Electricity

Reading A · Why Electricity Matters Again
AI、数据中心、电动车和工业转型为什么让电力重新决定产业布局？

For two centuries, industry has followed energy. Today that logic is returning in a new form: electrification is moving more transport, computing and industrial activity onto power systems. The winners will need electricity that is scalable, able to expand with demand, and reliable, available when factories need it. Countries with abundant power therefore gain an advantage that resembles the old advantage of having plentiful coal or oil.

🟣 A1 表达拆解

in a new form＝以新的形式；able to expand with demand 与 available when... 都是在原地解释前面的目标词。

🧠 A1 段落回忆

主旨：为什么今天的工业竞争又回到了“能源在哪里，产业就往哪里走”？

更多活动用电 → 用电需求扩大 → 工业需要既能扩张又稳定的供电。
expand with demand、available when needed、plentiful 分别直接给出三个词的语义范围。
electrification＝电气化；scalable＝可扩展的；reliable＝可靠的；abundant＝充足的。

Clean power, however, creates a timing problem. Solar and wind are intermittent: their output changes with sunlight and weather. Industry also values dispatchable sources that can produce power when operators call for it. Moving electricity from where it is generated to distant users requires transmission, while local distribution networks carry it through cities and industrial areas to the final customer.

🟣 A2 表达拆解

creates a timing problem 不是“时间有问题”，而是供电时间与需求时间可能错位；from where...to... 帮你区分远距离输电与本地配电。

🧠 A2 段落回忆

主旨：可再生电力为什么不能只看“发了多少电”？

风光随天气变化 → 需要可按需发电的来源 → 电还要从发电地送到用户。
output changes、produce when operators call、distant users、final customer 是四个定位线索。
intermittent＝间歇性的；dispatchable＝可调度的；transmission＝输电；distribution＝配电/分配。

One answer to the timing problem is storage, which keeps electricity for later use. Yet adding batteries alone does not guarantee fast deployment of new power projects. A shortage of grid connections can become a constraint on growth. Even after a plant is connected, too much electricity trying to pass through the same lines can create congestion, just as too many vehicles slow a crowded road.

🟣 A3 表达拆解

One answer to...＝解决……的一种办法；最后用“拥挤道路”类比电网，先理解现象再记术语。

🧠 A3 段落回忆

主旨：为什么“装更多电池”并不能自动解决供电问题？

电可以存下来 → 但项目仍要接入电网 → 接入不足限制增长 → 线路过载形成拥堵。
keeps electricity for later、fast projects、shortage limits growth、crowded road 是四个语境锚点。
storage＝储能；deployment＝部署/投用；constraint＝约束；congestion＝拥堵。

When the grid cannot accept all available power, operators may order curtailment, deliberately reducing output that could otherwise have been generated. This shows why installed capacity is not the same as usable electricity. The system must serve total load, meaning the demand placed on it, and it must survive the peak, the period when that demand reaches its highest level.

🟣 A4 表达拆解

is not the same as 是本段关键对比；meaning... 与后面的 the period when... 是典型的定义型猜词信号。

🧠 A4 段落回忆

主旨：为什么“装机容量很大”不等于“随时都有这么多电可用”？

电网接不下 → 主动少发电 → 装机≠可用电 → 系统还必须扛住最高需求。
deliberately reducing output、installed、demand placed on it、highest level 分别对应四词。
curtailment＝削减/限发；capacity＝容量；load＝负荷；peak＝峰值。

Factories traditionally valued baseload power that could run steadily for long periods. Now they must also manage price volatility, because electricity costs can rise and fall sharply. That makes affordability as important as physical supply: power that exists but is too expensive can still weaken industrial competitiveness against producers in lower-cost regions.

🟣 A5 表达拆解

as important as＝和……同样重要；power that exists but is too expensive 是“有电≠用得起”的让步结构。

🧠 A5 段落回忆

主旨：工业为什么不仅需要“有电”，还需要“电价可承受”？

工厂要稳定供电 → 电价又会波动 → 太贵即使有电也会削弱竞争能力。
run steadily、rise and fall sharply、too expensive、against producers 分别提供词义。
baseload＝基荷；volatility＝波动性；affordability＝可负担性；competitiveness＝竞争力。

This is why decarbonization is increasingly tied to industrial strategy rather than treated only as an environmental goal. Renewable sources such as wind and solar can add large amounts of low-carbon generation, but someone must plan and operate the system. A public or private utility may build networks and supply power, while long-term procurement contracts allow large users to secure electricity before new facilities are built.

🟣 A6 表达拆解

is tied to＝与……联系在一起；rather than treated only as＝而不是仅仅被视为；最后一句把“建设供给”和“提前采购”分开。

🧠 A6 段落回忆

主旨：为什么低碳转型已经变成工业战略的一部分？

低碳电源增加 → 需要有人建设运营系统 → 大用户还要提前锁定未来电力。
low-carbon goal、wind and solar、produce electricity、operate networks、secure electricity 对应五词。
decarbonization＝脱碳；renewable＝可再生的；generation＝发电；utility＝公用事业企业；procurement＝采购。
Reading B · Why Cheap Power Is Not Enough
拥有资源只是起点：真正决定产业能否落地的，是连接、融资与执行能力。
If electricity attracts industry, the next question is why some places with excellent energy resources still struggle to turn that advantage into factories and investment.

A project may have land and investors yet remain stuck in permitting, the process of obtaining official approval to build. It may then wait years for interconnection, the physical and contractual link to the grid. Networks also depend on equipment such as a transformer, which changes voltage, while old lines may need a retrofit—an upgrade added to existing infrastructure rather than a completely new system.

🟣 B1 表达拆解

remain stuck in＝长期卡在某环节；破折号后的 an upgrade added to existing... 是 retrofit 的直接定义。

🧠 B1 段落回忆

主旨：一个已经有土地和投资人的项目为什么仍可能多年无法用电？

先获批 → 再接网 → 还要关键设备 → 老旧设施可能需要升级。
official approval、link to grid、changes voltage、upgrade existing infrastructure 是直接释义。
permitting＝许可审批；interconnection＝并网/互联；transformer＝变压器；retrofit＝改造升级。

Once connected, industry cares about how much useful output it gets from each unit of energy. Higher efficiency means doing more with less input. Energy intensity, by contrast, measures how much energy is required for a unit of economic output. Reducing dependence on fossil fuels can lower exposure to fuel shocks, but replacing one source does not automatically eliminate energy dependency if critical equipment must still be imported.

🟣 B2 表达拆解

doing more with less 是 efficiency 的经典语义核心；by contrast 提醒你后面是在区分 efficiency 与 intensity。

🧠 B2 段落回忆

主旨：减少能源风险为什么不只是“少烧化石燃料”？

提高利用效率 → 降低单位产出的能源需求 → 少依赖化石燃料 → 但设备进口仍可能形成依赖。
more with less、energy per output、fuels、must still be imported 是四个语义支点。
efficiency＝效率；intensity＝强度；fossil＝化石的；dependency＝依赖。

That distinction matters for energy security. Heavy reliance on one fuel, supplier or technology creates vulnerability: a disruption in one place can hurt the whole system. Diversification reduces that exposure by spreading supply across several sources. But building alternatives requires governments and businesses to mobilize large pools of capital, and the cost of financing can determine whether a technically attractive project is actually built.

🟣 B3 表达拆解

reliance on one... 与 spreading...across several... 构成一对反义语境；whether...actually... 强调技术可行不等于商业落地。

🧠 B3 段落回忆

主旨：能源安全为什么同时是“来源结构”和“资金能力”的问题？

单一来源 → 容易受冲击 → 多元来源降低风险 → 但替代项目需要筹集资金。
disruption hurts whole system、several sources、large pools of capital、cost of capital 对应四词。
vulnerability＝脆弱性；diversification＝多元化；mobilize＝调动/筹集；financing＝融资。

Investors therefore ask whether a project is bankable—credible enough to attract lenders and capital. Its commercial viability depends on expected revenue, costs and risk. High interest rates can become a financial hurdle, especially where borrowing is expensive. If grid construction then begins to lag behind electricity demand, even excellent renewable resources may fail to translate into industrial growth.

🟣 B4 表达拆解

credible enough to... 是 bankable 的定义；fail to translate into＝未能转化为，是阅读中常见的“条件没有变成结果”。

🧠 B4 段落回忆

主旨：为什么“技术上能建”与“现实中建成”之间还有很大距离？

项目要能吸引资本 → 收益风险要成立 → 高利率形成障碍 → 电网建设若落后，资源优势无法兑现。
attract lenders、revenue/cost/risk、obstacle、behind demand 分别限定四词。
bankable＝具融资吸引力的；viability＝可行性/生存能力；hurdle＝障碍；lag＝落后。

This is why countries trying to accelerate electrification must do more than announce generation targets. Grid expansion has to occur before shortages become severe. New factories and data centres may form an industrial cluster around dependable power, changing economic geography as investment shifts toward places where electricity, networks and skilled workers can be combined.

🟣 B5 表达拆解

do more than＝不能只做……；as investment shifts toward... 说明“经济地理变化”具体指资本向某些地点重新集中。

🧠 B5 段落回忆

主旨：电力基础设施怎样改变产业地图？

加快电气化 → 电网提前扩建 → 企业围绕可靠电力聚集 → 投资地点发生变化。
speed up、grid gets larger、factories form together、investment shifts places 对应四词。
accelerate＝加速；expansion＝扩张；cluster＝集群；geography＝地理格局。

In the end, natural resources are only one part of the advantage. Proximity to cheap power helps because shorter distance can reduce connection costs, but capable institutions are needed to turn plans into projects. They must allocate scarce grid capacity among competing users, design an incentive that rewards useful investment, and improve coordination among generators, networks, financiers and industrial customers. The new industrial race is therefore not simply about who has energy, but who can organize it into a dependable economic system.

🟣 B6 表达拆解

turn plans into projects＝把规划真正变成项目；not simply A, but B 是全文结论结构：关键不只是拥有能源，而是组织和兑现能力。

🧠 B6 段落回忆

主旨：全文最终认为真正的工业电力优势是什么？

靠近便宜电力只是起点 → 制度负责分配稀缺资源 → 激励投资 → 协调多方 → 才形成可靠系统。
shorter distance、organizations/rules、scarce capacity among users、rewards investment、among many actors 对应五词。
proximity＝接近/邻近；institution＝制度/机构；allocate＝分配；incentive＝激励；coordination＝协调。
